=== The Ballad of Mount Kyllang and Mount Symper (Ka Jingrwai U Lum Kyllang bad U Lum Symper) ===
Composer: Ancient Oral Lore / Bardic Verses (Traditional)
Category: Epic Mythological Ballad

--- KHASI LYRICS ---
U Lum Kyllang bad u Lum Symper,
Ki arlei kynrem ha ka Ri Khasi;
Ki la ialeh ban pyni bor,
Ban tip uba kham jrong halor pyrthei!

U Kyllang u la pynher ia ki mawsiang,
U Symper u la kyntait da ka khyndew;
Ki riat ki la kynkhih, ka bneng ka la sawa,
Haduh ba u Kyllang u la jop ha ka nam!

Hynrei mynta ki shong suk ha la ri,
Ki sakhi ia ka nam jong u Hynniewtrep!

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION ---
Mount Kyllang and Mount Symper,
Two colossal brothers upon the Khasi hills;
They contended fiercely to prove their might,
To see who was tallest and proudest beneath the heavens!

Kyllang hurled massive granite boulders through the sky,
Symper flung back sods of red highland earth;
The ravines shook, the heavens echoed with the thunder,
Until Kyllang stood triumphant in enduring granite glory!

Yet today both mountains rest in peace across our land,
Eternal witnesses to the greatness of the Children of the Seven Huts!
