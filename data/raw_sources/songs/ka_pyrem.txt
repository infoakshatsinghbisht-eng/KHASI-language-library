=== Vernal Awakening (Ka Pyrem) (Ka Jingrwai jong Ka Pyrem) ===
Composer: H.W. Sten (1962)
Category: Spring Celebration & Floral Lyric

--- KHASI LYRICS ---
Ha ki riat ba rben ki syntiew ki phuh,
Ka Pyrem ka la wan ban pynkmen ia ngi!
U tiew-doh-maw u la pynpaw la ka khmat,
Ka sngi ka la shai ha ka dur ba phyrnai.

Ki sim-tyngwieng ki rwai ha ki tnat dieng,
Ka lyer-pyrem ka beh ha ka sur ba bang;
Ko samla ba shlur, to mih noh sha rep,
Ba ka ri kan ai ia ka spah ba paka!

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION ---
Along the steep rocky gorges the mountain flowers bloom,
Sweet Spring has arrived to gladden our hearts!
The wild rock orchid reveals its radiant face,
The morning sun shines in brilliant resplendence.

The swallows warble upon leafy branches,
The gentle spring breeze wafts in delicious sweetness;
O brave young cultivators, go forth unto the fields,
That our beloved homeland may yield her richest bounty!
