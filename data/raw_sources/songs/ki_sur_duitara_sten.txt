iv PREFACE  TO  THE  SECOND  EDITION 
THIS  BOOK  is  written  according  to  the  syllabus  of 
the  High  School  Leaving  Certificate  Examination.  In  pre- 
paring this  book,  care  has  been  taken  to  make  it  meet  the 
need  of  my  friends  in  Khasi  and  Jaintia  Hills  who  prepare 
themselves  for  the  HSLC  Examination  of  the  Board  of 
Secondary  Education,  Assam.  On  the  other  hand,  friends 
preparing  for  one  of  the  following  examinations,  namely, 
Senior  Basic  School  Examination;  Junior  and  Senior 
Teachers’  Examination  of  the  Basic  Education  Training 
Centres  in  Khasi  -   Jaintia  Hills;  and  Teachers’  Examina- 
tion of  the  Normal  Training  School,  Cherrapunjee  will  find 
this  book  indispensable. 
In  this  edition,  notes  on  six  of  the  pieces  as  translated 
by  Mr.  Tham  from  fiom  which  are  being  read  in 
B.A.  classes,  also  have  been  included.  Teachers  teaching 
the  pieces  treated  of  in  this  book  will  find  it  helpful. 
The  first  edition  was  brought  out  in  1962  under  the 
title  :   Matric.  Khasi  Poetry  Made  Easy;  I   realise  that  some 
of  those  notes  in  the  first  edition  are  not  sufficient  to  serve 
as  answers  to  the  questions  set  in  the  examination.  What- 
ever the  standard,  in  bringing  out  the  edition,  I   have  made 
myself  a   pioneer  in  the  field  of  preparation  of  notes  on 
these  poems.  The  fact  that  the  present  edition  is  called  for 
shows  that  more  friends  are  added  to  me  every  year.  Rea- 
lising their  needs,  I   have  included  herein  university  and 
Board  questions  on  Ka  Duitara  Ksiar  set  in  the  years  1962 
to  1966  with  mode]  answers. 
In  pieces  like  “Kynjai  Ha  La  Ri,”  “Ki  Khun  Ka 
Mariang”  and  “U  Lok  Bashisha”,  instead  of  summaries, substances  or  gists,  my  friends  will  on  the  other  hand  feel, 
as  if  they  were  in  my  class,  because  I   deliver  a   lecture  in- 
stead of  dictating  notes  to  them.

PREFACE  TO  THE  THIRD  EDITION 
THIS  BOOK  was  out  of  print  for  eleven  years.  I 
had  not  forgotten  my  readers  completely,  but  new  reasons 
overpowered  me  these  years  and  took  me  to  hitherto 
untrodden  regions  of  our  literature.  I   am  confident  that 
works  produced  by  me  during  this  long  interregnum  have 
benefitted  our  Khasi  reading  public. 
This  edition  differs  greatly  from  the  second  one.  Poems 
as  were  not  composed  by  Soso  Tham,  his  translation,  and 
the  guidance  for  approaching  examination  questions  have 
all  been  excluded.  New  poems  have  on  the  other  hand 
been  criticised.  I   must  state  clearly  that  students  may  not 
find  much  help  in  the  present  work  because  the  book  was 
prepared  with  a   specific  purpose  ;   to  train  the  mind  of  the 
readers  for  further  research  about  the  poet.  Moreover,  the 
special  held  of  study  in  this  work  is  to  examine  the  influen- 
ce of  other  poets  and  writers  on  Soso  Tham.  Added  to 
those  changes  effected,  a   few  hundred  copies  of  the  book 
are  being  bound  with  two  others,  though  the  third  one 
which  I   completed  first  is  yet  to  see  the  light  of  the  sun. 
I   suggest  to  my  readers  that  they  should  not  loose  heart 
right  now  beoause  they  will  in  due  course  realize  the 
importance  of  the  present  edition,  it  may  be  at  the  degree 
course,  and  it  may  be  at  the  time  when  they  prepare  for 
their  doctoral  theses  alsoi 
The  loss  of  the  earlier  features  has  been  compensated 
by  detailed  discussion  of  facts  as  those  are  revealed  to  the 
author  by  the  poems  themselves.  I   am  sure  that  with  this 
edition,  the  book  will  not  escape  the  attention  of  literary 
historians  any  more. 
Garikhana,  Shillong, 
October  11,  1979. 
H.W.  Sten
