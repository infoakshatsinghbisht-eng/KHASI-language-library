=== Song of the Four Seasons (Ki Saw Aiom) (Ki Saw Aiom: Pyrem, Lyiur, Synrai, Tlang) ===
Composer: Soso Tham / H.W. Sten (1962)
Category: Seasonal Pastoral & Nature Cycle

--- KHASI LYRICS ---
Ka Pyrem ka la wan ryngkat u tiew kulab,
Ki sim ki la rwai ha ki tnat ba jyrngam;
Ka Mariang ka la pynphuh ia la ka sopti,
Ki samla ki la mih ban rep ha madan!

Ka Lyiur ka la wan ryngkat ka slapbah,
Ki pyrthat ki la rih, ki kshaid ki la sawa;
Ki thwei ki la shlei, ki um ki la tuid,
Ka ri lum ka la pyngngad baroh shi lyiur!

Ka Synrai ka la wan ryngkat u kba ksiar,
Ki lyngkha ki la tiew ha ka rong ba sngur;
Ki tymmen ki kmen, ki khunlung ki rkhie,
Ngin lum ia u kba sha ki thiar ba khraw!

U Tlang u la wan ryngkat ka erkyrkha,
Ka thah ka la sop ia ki phlang ha madan;
Ha rympei ngin shong ban sngap ia ka parom,
Ki ksing bad ki sharati kin sawa pat ha pyrem!

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION ---
Springtime has arrived accompanied by wild mountain roses,
The forest birds sing upon verdant leafy boughs;
Mother Nature has arrayed her resplendent mantle,
And the youth step forth to cultivate the fertile fields!

Summer Monsoon has arrived with torrential rains,
The thunder rumbles deep, the waterfalls roar across the cliffs;
The gorges overflow, crystal waters tumble free,
And the mountain land rests cool throughout the wet season!

Autumn has arrived laden with golden ripened paddy,
The terraced fields glow in pure radiant amber hue;
The elders rejoice, the children laugh in delight,
As we gather the harvest into the great ancestral granaries!

Winter has arrived with chilly mountain winds,
White frost carpets the meadow grasses in the dawn;
Beside the hearth fire we gather to hear the ancient lore,
Until drums and sweet flutes resound once more in Spring!
