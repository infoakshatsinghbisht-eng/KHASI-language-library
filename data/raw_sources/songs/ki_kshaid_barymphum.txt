=== The Cascading Waterfalls of Ri Hynniewtrep (Ki Kshaid Barymphum) (Ki Kshaid Barymphum) ===
Composer: H.W. Sten (1979)
Category: Nature Ballad & Geography Hymn

--- KHASI LYRICS ---
Na ki riat ba jrong ki kshaid ki la noh,
Kum ki paila rupa ki jaw ruid ruid;
Ka Nohkalikai ka kynih ha ka them,
Ka Dainthlen ka phet ha ki mawsiang ba khraw!

Ki barymphum ksiar ki ieng ha ka suin,
Ka lyer ka pynkhih ia ka jingshai ka sngi;
Ki um ba kordor ki pynim ia ka ri,
Ka nam jong ki kshaid kan neh ha la tnum!

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION ---
From dizzying rocky precipices the waterfalls plunge,
Like cascading necklaces of shimmering silver beads;
Nohkalikai thunders into the deep forested basin,
Dainthlen races across ancient flat river boulders!

Rainbows of shimmering gold arch across the misty air,
The mountain breeze dances with the sunlight;
These precious living waters sustain our entire land,
The renown of these cataracts shall endure forever!
