=== Serenity in Our Homeland (Kynjai Ha La Ri) (Kynjai Ha La Ri) ===
Composer: Soso Tham / H.W. Sten (1936)
Category: Bardic Lyric & Homeland Elegy

--- KHASI LYRICS ---
Kynjai ha la ri, ha la tnum ba pyngngad,
Ym don ba khuslai, ym don ba pynsalat;
Ki dieng kseh ki kynih ha ka erpyngngad,
Ka dohnud ka kmen ha ka suk ba kynjai.

Ki wahduid ki tuid da ka sur ba jemnud,
Ki mawsiang ki sngap ia ka parom mynhyndai;
Ha tnum u Lum Shillong ka lyer ka dang beh,
Ka nam jong ka Ri kan ym ju klet noh!

La ngi long kiba duk, kiba rit ha pyrthei,
Ha la ri ngi long kiba kmen bad shongkhrein;
U kba u la heh ha ki lyngkha ba iar,
U Duitara u tem ha ka sur ba phyrnai!

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION ---
Serenity in our own land, beneath our cool highland roof,
No sorrow troubles us, no harsh distraction disturbs our peace;
The tall pine trees murmur in the refreshing mountain breeze,
The heart rejoices in unhurried, tranquil serenity.

The clear mountain brooks ripple with a gentle melody,
The ancient granite monoliths listen to legends of old;
Upon the ridge of Shillong Peak the breeze still blows soft,
The renown of our homeland shall never be forgotten!

Though we be humble and small in the eyes of the wide world,
In our native hills we dwell majestic and content;
The golden paddy stands tall across wide terraced fields,
And the golden Duitara plucks its shimmering melody!
