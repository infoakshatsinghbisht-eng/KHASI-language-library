=== Days of Yore (Ki Sngi Ba La Leit Noh) (Ki Sngi Ba La Leit Noh) ===
Composer: H.W. Sten (1979)
Category: Elegiac Ballad & Ancestral Memory

--- KHASI LYRICS ---
Kumno ngan klet ia kito ki sngi,
Ba nga dang ialehkai ha la ka shnong;
Ki sngi ba shai, ki por ba suk,
Haba ka dohnud ka dang lui-lui!

Ki tymmen mynhyndai ki iathuh parom,
Ha rympei dingshan ha ka miet ba daitthah;
La ki sngi ki la leit noh kum ka lyer,
Ka jingkynmaw ia ki kan ym duh lano lano!

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION ---
How can I ever forget those golden days of old,
When I used to play innocently in my native village;
Those bright days, those peaceful times,
When the youthful heart knew only gentle innocence!

The elders of ancient times shared beloved folklore,
Beside the warm hearth on bitterly cold frosty nights;
Though those days have drifted away like the passing mountain wind,
Their sacred memory shall never, ever fade away!
