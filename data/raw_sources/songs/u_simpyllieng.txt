=== The Mountain Cuckoo (U Simpyllieng) (Ka Sur U Simpyllieng) ===
Composer: Soso Tham (1925)
Category: Pastoral Birdsong & Folk Ballad

--- KHASI LYRICS ---
Ha khap ki lum u kynih jam,
Ko simpyllieng ba rwai sngewtynnad!
Ka sur jong me ka pynkyndit ia ri,
Ban thung ia u kba ha ki lyngkha!

Me pah na jngai ha ki khlaw ba rben,
Ka lyer ka kit ia ka sur sha ki them;
Ki tymmen ki sngap, ki samla ki mih,
Ka por ban rep ka la poi pat ha ri!

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION ---
Along the forested rim of the mountains it calls clearly,
The gentle cuckoo singing its delightful tune!
Thy melodious cadence awakens the whole land,
To sow the seed-paddy across terraced slopes!

Thou callest from afar within the dense sacred forests,
The mountain wind carries thy song down to the valleys;
The elders pause to listen, the youth rise up to labor,
The blessed time for planting has returned to our hills!
