=== The Folk Humor of the Simpleton (Ka Biria u Biej) (Ka Biria u Biej ha Shnong) ===
Composer: Traditional Village Storytellers / H.W. Sten (1962)
Category: Folk Comic Satire & Village Phawar

--- KHASI LYRICS ---
U biej u la leit sha Iewduh ban thied jain,
U la phong da ka tupia ba jrong kum u phlang;
U kheiñ ialade u riewstad u riewbha,
Pynban u la thut ha ka madan Iewbah!

Ki samla ki la rkhie kynriah kylleng,
Ki tymmen ki la khih la ka khlieh mian-mian;
Ai ngin rkhie, hynrei ngin kynmaw ia ka akor,
Ba ka jingshongkhrein ka jah lada khlem jingstad!

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION ---
The simpleton went down to Iewduh market to buy grand clothes,
He donned a tall comical hat like a stalk of dry reed;
He deemed himself a scholar and a man of great nobility,
Yet he stumbled headlong on the stones of the big market!

The market youth burst out into roaring laughter all around,
The wise elders gently shook their heads with amusement;
Let us laugh in good humor, yet remember proper modesty,
For false pride vanishes quickly where true wisdom is lacking!
