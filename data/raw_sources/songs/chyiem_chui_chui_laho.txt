=== Pnar Laho Dance Song (Chyiem Chui-Chui) (Chyiem Chui-Chui: Rwai Shad Laho) ===
Composer: Jaintia / Pnar Oral Tradition (Traditional Ancient)
Category: Pnar Folk Dance Song (Laho)

--- KHASI LYRICS ---
Chyiem chui-chui, chyiem chui-chui!
Ia kynjoh kti ban shad ha madan;
Ka kynthei hapdeng, ki shynrang harud,
Ha ka sur Laho ba sawa thiang!

U shyngwiang u pyrsad ha ki lum Jowai,
Ki khun ka Ri Jaintia ki la kmen;
Khlem ksing khlem nakra ngi shad da ka sur,
Ka akor ba bha ngin pynneh ha tnum!

Chyiem chui-chui, ia kynjoh ban kmen,
Ka nam u Pnar kan neh ha pyrthei!

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION ---
Chyiem chui-chui, chyiem chui-chui!
Link hands together to dance upon the field;
The maiden in the center, the young men on either flank,
Stepping to the sweet melodious cadence of Laho!

The bamboo shyngwiang flute breathes upon the hills of Jowai,
The sons and daughters of Jaintia rejoice in unity;
Without heavy drums or kettle-drums we dance to human melody,
Preserving our honorable ancestral traditions forever!

Chyiem chui-chui, step together in high delight,
The honorable name of the Pnar shall endure across the earth!
