=== The Fragrant Highland Wind (Ka Lyer Ka Dang Sieng) (Ka Lyer Ka Dang Sieng ha ki Lum) ===
Composer: H.W. Sten (1979)
Category: Sensory Pastoral Lyric

--- KHASI LYRICS ---
Ha ki tnum ba jrong ka lyer ka dang sieng,
Ka iathuh ia ka nam jong ka Ri ba pyngngad;
Ka sieng u dieng kseh, ka sieng u tiew-khlaw,
Ka pynim ia ka dohnud ba thait ha lynti!

Ha ki lynti-maw ba la tei ki tymmen,
Ngi iaid da ka kmen ryngkat ka sur rwai;
Ka lyer ka dang beh, ka sngi ka dang phyrnai,
Ka Ri Khasi kan neh ha ka suk ba kynjai!

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION ---
Across the lofty ridges the wind still wafts sweet fragrance,
Proclaiming the glory of our cool, blessed homeland;
Scent of green highland pines, scent of wild mountain orchids,
Reviving the weary heart along the rugged trail!

Along ancient paved stone paths constructed by our ancestors,
We journey with rejoicing and songs upon our lips;
The mountain breeze still blows, the sun shines bright,
The Khasi homeland shall abide in peaceful serenity!
