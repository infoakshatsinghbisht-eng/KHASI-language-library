=== The Morning Star of Hope (U Khlur) (U Khlur Shai Jingkyrmen) ===
Composer: Soso Tham / H.W. Sten (1936)
Category: Lyrical Allegory & Celestial Song

--- KHASI LYRICS ---
Ha ka suin ba iong me tyngshain marwei,
Ko Khlur ba shai jong ka step-phyrngab!
Me pyni ia ka lynti ia ki nongiaid-wir,
Me ring ia ka jingshai ban pynpeh ia ka dum!

La ki lyoh ki tap ia ka pyrthei baroh,
Me ieng triang khlem khih khlem phet;
Ka jingkyrmen jong ngi me pynim pat sa shisien,
Ko Khlur ba thiang jong ka Ri Khasi!

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION ---
Across the dark velvet firmament thou shinest alone,
O radiant Morning Star of early dawn!
Thou pointest out the hidden trail to weary wanderers,
Drawing forth the morning light to scatter every shadow!

Though dense mountain clouds shroud the sleeping world,
Thou standest steadfast, unmoved and unwavering;
Thou revivest our hope once more with radiant dawn,
O sweet, pure Star of the Khasi hills!
