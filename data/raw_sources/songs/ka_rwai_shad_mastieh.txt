=== Warrior Sword Dance Chant (Ka Rwai Shad Mastieh) (Ka Rwai Shad Mastieh Ki Riewshlur) ===
Composer: Traditional Clan Syiemship War Lore (Traditional Ancient)
Category: Warrior Dance & Heroic Phawar

--- KHASI LYRICS ---
Kren ka Bom, sawa ka Tangmuri!
Ki samla shlur ki la ieng kynhoi;
Ka waitlam ha ka kti kamtrei,
Ka symphiah ba thaba ha ka kti kadiang!

Ngin iada ia ka Ri, ngin da ia ka Kur,
Ia ka Hok ngin ym ju aili ha ka khim!
Lada u nongshun u wan ban thombor,
Ka dohnud ba shlur kan ieng ha ka nam!

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION ---
The giant bass drum thunders, the reed horn cries aloud!
The valiant warriors arise with a battle cheer;
The flashing steel sword brandished in the right hand,
The white whisk gleaming in the left hand!

We shall defend the homeland, we shall shield the Clan,
Never shall we yield Righteousness in times of peril!
If the enemy comes to oppress our sovereign soil,
Our brave hearts shall stand victorious in honorable renown!
