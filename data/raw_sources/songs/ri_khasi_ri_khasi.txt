=== Patriotic Anthem of Khasi Land (Ri Khasi, Ri Khasi) (Ri Khasi, Ri Khasi, Nga Ieid Ia Pha) ===
Composer: Rev. Dr. John Roberts / D.S. Khongdup (1895)
Category: Patriotic Anthem & National Song

--- KHASI LYRICS ---
Ri Khasi, Ri Khasi, nga ieid ïa pha,
Ka Ri kaba ieid uba rim uba jah;
Ki tymmen mynhyndai la ngam ïohi shuh,
Jingkynmaw ïa ki kan ym duh.

Lum Khasi, Lum Khasi, kat phi ym ju don,
Ka lyer ba sngewtynnad ngi bam katba mon;
Ka um kaba shngiam ka tuid ha ki wah,
Kam rngad myntlang ruh ka sah.

Lang:—
Ri, Ri, nga ieit thoin thoin la ka ri!
Ngin kiew sha khlieh, sha phrang ngin mih,
Umpohliew jingstad ngin ïadih!

Ki lum ba pyngngad, ki them ba jyrngam,
Ha tnum jong ki ngi ieng da ka nam;
Ka Hok bad ka Sot ngin kdup ha ka shadem,
Ha pyrthei baroh ka nam kan heh kmen!

Shaphrang ngin kiew raieh raidain,
Sha tlong ba mih ki umpohliew,
Jingstad ngin dih ha kliar sangaria,
Ko Meirilung, ki khun ki ksiew!

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION ---
Ri Khasi, Ri Khasi, I love thee dearly,
Beloved homeland of our ancient ancestors who have gone before;
Though our eyes behold no longer the elders of old,
The living memory of them shall never fade away.

O Khasi Mountains, Khasi Hills, none can compare with thee,
The sweet bracing mountain breeze we breathe as freely as we desire;
The crystal pure water flows sweetly down the rocky streams,
It never dries up, remaining cool even through winter's chill.

Chorus:—
Homeland, O Homeland, with tender deepest devotion I love my native land!
We shall climb to the summit, forward we shall march,
From the deep fountain of wisdom we all shall drink!

Cool highlands of peace, emerald green valleys below,
Upon thy sacred soil we stand with honored renown;
Righteousness and Truth we embrace within our breasts,
Across the entire world our fame shall echo in joy!

Forward we shall advance, shoulder to shoulder,
Unto the sacred source where eternal fountains spring,
Wisdom we shall drink upon the highest summits,
O Mother Land, for all thy children and descendants!
