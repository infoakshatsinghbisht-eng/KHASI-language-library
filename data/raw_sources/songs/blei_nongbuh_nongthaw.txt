=== Hymn to the Supreme Creator (Mynsiem Bakhuid, Ko Blei Nongbuh Nongthaw) (Mynsiem Bakhuid, Ko Blei Nongbuh Nongthaw) ===
Composer: Classical Khasi Sacred Hymnody (LB 168 / Seng Khasi) (Traditional Sacred)
Category: Sacred Spiritual Hymn & Invocation

--- KHASI LYRICS ---
Mynsiem Bakhuid, ko Blei Nongbuh Nongthaw,
Wan ban shongsah bad ki khun batlot bor;
Theh sbun ha ngi jing-aiei jingkyrkhu,
Thaw thymmai pat, maramot ia ngi Trai.
To wan ko Trai, wan pynkhlain, wan aibor,
Wan ban hikai, lam ia ngi sha jingshai!

Ko Nongpyntngen b’la kular U Jisu,
Me long jingai bakordor jong U Kpa;
Me iai thehsei jingai hynniew syrtap,
Ka jingtip-briew jingtip-blei Me pynsan.
To wan ko Trai, wan pynkhlain, wan aibor,
Wan ban hikai, lam ia ngi sha jingshai!

Ko thylliej ding, wan hiar halor jong ngi,
Thang duh ka pop, sait noh ki jingthohbria;
Phrang pat ia ngi ha ka jingieid nylla,
Ba ngin iai long ki khun ba-ieid i’u Kpa.
To wan ko Trai, wan pynkhlain, wan aibor,
Wan ban hikai, lam ia ngi sha jingshai!

Ko Bor a-neng, beh krad i’u ksuid u khrei,
Ia ngi to ai ka jingsuk ba-longblei;
To long ma Me U Nongkdup Nongkynshew,
To lam ma Me ha lynti ka jingim.
To wan ko Trai, wan pynkhlain, wan aibor,
Wan ban hikai, lam ia ngi sha jingshai!

Thymmei jingstad, lam ia ngi ban shemphang,
Ban tip i’u Kpa bad i’u Khun bamarwei,
Lem bad Me ruh, ko Mynsiem jong U Blei,
Burom, ka nam tang ia Me ngin iai tei.
To wan ko Trai, wan pynkhlain, wan aibor,
Wan ban hikai, lam ia ngi sha jingshai!

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION ---
O Holy Spirit, O God Creator and Architect of all,
Come and abide with Thy children frail in strength;
Pour out graciously upon us Thy grace and blessings,
Recreate us anew, restore and renew us, O Lord!
Come, O Lord, come strengthen and impart power,
Come teach and lead us into the divine light!

O Comforter promised through divine grace,
Thou art the most precious gift of the Father;
Continually pouring forth sevenfold heavenly gifts,
Nurturing within us the knowledge of God and humanity.
Come, O Lord, come strengthen and impart power,
Come teach and lead us into the divine light!

O tongue of fire, descend upon our midst,
Burn away every blemish, cleanse all defilement;
Refine us once more in genuine, purest love,
That we may ever remain cherished children of the Father.
Come, O Lord, come strengthen and impart power,
Come teach and lead us into the divine light!

O Heavenly Power, drive far away all evil and discord,
Grant unto us divine tranquility and peace;
Be Thou our sheltering Guardian and divine Sustainer,
Guide our footsteps along the straight path of life.
Come, O Lord, come strengthen and impart power,
Come teach and lead us into the divine light!

O Fountainhead of wisdom, enlighten our understanding,
To know the eternal Creator in truth and purity,
Together with Thee, O Holy Spirit of God,
Honor and glory to Thy name alone shall we ever raise!
Come, O Lord, come strengthen and impart power,
Come teach and lead us into the divine light!
