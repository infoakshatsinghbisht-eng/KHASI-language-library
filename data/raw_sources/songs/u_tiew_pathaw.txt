=== The Wild Mountain Blossom (U Tiew Pathaw) (U Tiew Pathaw ha Ri Lum) ===
Composer: H.W. Sten (1979)
Category: Folk Lyric & Flora Metaphor

--- KHASI LYRICS ---
Ha ki mawsiang ba eh me mih khlem pisa,
Ko tiew-pathaw ba stem kum ka ksiar!
La ka erlang ka shoh, la ka slap ka theh,
Me rkhie pynban ha la ka jinglong!

Ym don ba thung, ym don ba sumar,
U Blei Nongthaw u ai ia ka bor;
Kumta to long ka akor jong ngi,
Ban neh triang ha ka Hok bad ka Sot!

--- ENGLISH TRANSLATION ---
Upon hard granite boulders thou sprouteth without complaint,
O wild blossom glowing golden like pure amber!
Though biting gale winds strike and heavy rains pour down,
Thou smilest still in gentle contentment!

No human hand planted thee, no gardener tended thee,
God the Creator alone imparted thy strength;
Even so let our moral character stand,
Steadfast forever in Righteousness and Truth!
