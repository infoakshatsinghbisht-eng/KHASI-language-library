AgANSOaNI :-
Ia kan'dikgipa dakmesokaniko anga A'chikku-o sea-
jotani bidingo komibeenga ine nike, maidakode iaba gital
segnigiparangna didiatani ong'gen jotton ka'bee seaha.
Iako anga Bihar-o jasana donge okkri-cha'asigiparangna
kam ka'engmitingo saksan  sikdikdikgipa somoirangko
jakkale seaha. ( May, 15th-June 15, 1967 ). Indiba iachi
anga chu'sokbee seaha. ine aganna man'ja maina chu'sokgipa
ong'nara jringjring sena ranta ka'achisa mana ama. I an
chusokgipa seani ong'a ine anga aganna man'ja.
Iano bang'gija dakmesokgnigiparanko done ramram
janggl tangbewaloniko ra'e seengon anga darangkoba mik-
songe janapjaha are bimungrangko jakkalon chanchichipesan
donaiaha.
Ia dakmesokaniko agangope pante saksani maiba
a'selchi nokoni ong'katange bilsi ruutbee gipino dongenba,
ma'a mamung dakchakaniko man'jaenba chonbea dokan
ge'sako demehik baksa kulie obostachi draataniko man'e
kragija kamrangchi janggi tangna nangaha. Indake ong'engon
salsao ua re'bapiloa saksaba saksako uigrikjae je obostao
janggi tangengachim apsanon ga'akaha. Jensalo ma'a detangko
ma'sioa ja'manchakdugaaha. Indake jechi nokdangko rikaha,
uchin bon'angaha.
Iake chu'sokatanio anga bang'bea ripengrangni ka'don-
gataniko aro didiataniko man'aha aro anga uamang pilaknan
gro nanggipa ong'a. Anga angn chatri saksa Miss Barbothy
K. Marak-ko mittelbena nanga jean somoi dongzijaku drae
iako type ka'na dakchakaha,
