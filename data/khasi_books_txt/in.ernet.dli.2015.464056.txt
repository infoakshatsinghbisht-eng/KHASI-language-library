JANAPCHENGANI
A.chik a-songo kitap sena changgiparang bang-heen dongaha
indiba ku-tangchi segimin kitaprangko poraina sikgiparang bangjani
gimin bang-batan sena siktokja. lan an chingni ku-tangna ka-sagijaniko
mesokani gita ong a. Anga ia Novel-ko ruuhea salrangna skangan
scahachim indiba adita ncng-nikanirangchi chapa kaatningna man jaha.
la segunin golpo angni gisikiangoniko tarie seanisan ongaia enia jan
kitapna ka'sagiparangna poraitobegipa ba jakkaltohegipa ongjanaba
donga. lan adita manderangni nangnikaniko gisikona sokate angna
ka donganiko on-ahanichi chapa ka-ataniona sokaha.
lano janapgimin song ba mandeni bimingrang badiaba biap ba
nandeko minga baksa apsan ong'genchimode uan maibakai
ong gakanisan ongaigen, maina ian golpoo hak ra-giparangko dingtang
maekate mesoknasan uarangko donaha. la segimin golpoo darang
a'gilsakni mandeni daka rikako ba kam cholonko nirikie uko janapna
miksonganirangha dongja indiba basakoba saniha janggi tanganiona
indaka obostarang sokbanaba donga. indake sakgipin manderangni
dakchaka gri saksan ong-engmittingo iano janapgimin kattarangchi una
ka-dimeataniko on na man aba donga ine mangsonganinasa ong'a.
Anga iano adita kata gitalrangkoba jakkalaha. jekai garini palo
kolre., kitapni palo chapmong, koborni palo knanku enia uandake
gipinrang. A-chikni minganio bang-an miksongani gnang bimingrangko
donaha, jekai. matchu ha matnokrangni gisepo chubatgipa, matcha ba
matshako cha'gipa. Ulandake biterangonikoha te-rik ba rongsa
ronggipinko rika gita nanggipa bite, te-gatchu ba a-oni chukalao gata
gita nanggipa bite ine mniksonga gnang minge jakkala. Indake bimangni
kri an-ching A-chik ku'siko dongkugijagipa kattarangko taridape jatni
agananiko apalatna man'gen.
Bon-kame iako matchotsrange chusokatna dakchakahanina

AGANDAPANI
A-chikrangoba sena-jotna changgiparang bang'en gnang.
Saobarang iana ba-kapgipa gunrangba gnang, indioba basakobade
an'tangtangari dal-droe silrorona neng-a. Uaranga basakoba o-krie,
chiru-a:gate. dilneng-do-mikako man'ode silrorona aro namgipa
biterangko nangna man'a. An'ching uandakgiparangko
an-tangtangari dal-ako nie dongaigija nirok-simsake nambatatna
nanga. Patigimin ba-kapgipa gunrangko nambatatna A-chikjat
sakantian dakchakanirangko on:paode nambea.
Gipin ku-sikrango bang'a dingtang dingtang rokomni sea-
jotanirang (literary genre) donggenchimoba A-chik ku'siko
seanirangde namen komibcnga aro badiaba rokomni sea-
jotanirangde dongsrangkuja ine aganoba gualani dongjawa. Sea-
jotani rokomrangoni Dakmesokna seani (Drama) aro gisiko golpo
banaidrae Songsarni golpoko seani (Novel), uarangba
mongsonggipa sea-jotanirang ong'a. Uarangko college aro uni-
versity-rango ra'naba nanga.
A-chikkuchi segimin songsarni golpo dao dipet Redin
Mominni "Khalsin aro Sonatchi", Simison R. Sangmani
"Swarmalata"-oniko pe-gipa "Sonabal Me-chik", Icylian R. Marakni
"Chigitchakgreni Nokma" aro “"Gamseng", unbaksana da'o gital
Prabodh M. Sangmani “"Parakgija Ka'saa" aro “Dikreni Gitcham
Poto" iarangsan ongkuainga.
Chasong damberangoni Te. Barnath A. Sangma songsarni
golpo "SIKIME"-ko seaha. lan chadamberangoni ia rokomni
seanirangna re-mikkangani aro jaku de-ani ong'enga. Ua iano
gisiktango golpoko tarie ba gisiko golpoko rike uno bak ra'giparang
(Character) -koba ia chasongoni ong-bewalo dakataha. Songsarni
golpo badiaba chasongo ong'a obostako janeraogita nikatna
neck-tie kae juta gananiara ia chasongna ka-dingstekani ong'naba
donga, indiba uko segipa mandeni chasongo mai ong-a uko photo-
rangoniko nikode ong-bebea ine nikna man-a.

A-chik jatni dakrong-ka-rongkugijagipa jekon da-alonaba
A.chikrang kae chakujaenga, uan juta sikani aro knirate
cha'anirang ong'a, uarangkoba ua ia songsarni golpoo mesoke
on'aha. Beben A-chik me-chikrangoni me-chikskako kni ratani.
mikkang tariani kamrangko ka'e janggitanggiparangba dongengaha.
Minggipin ua iano ku-gipinchi minggimin bimungrangko
ku-tango maidake banaie mingode ba donode namgenchim ba
kragenchim uarangkoba banaie jakkale mesokaha. Unigimin
saobarang ua A-chikkuona banaigimin bimungrangko krae nikjaoba
'ong'niksrangja' ine jeaigija krae nikako una uiatode nambea.
Ku'tango sal, ja mingani dongoba anching kugipinko jakkale
ku'tangchi mai minga ukode uija aro aganrongja. Uarangko da-alo
A-chikkuchi segipa songbadrango aro kitaprango jakkale
mesokode, uarangko poraie gisikona chinaproroe knalo uko
jatgimik jakkalna man'gen.

ong-a. Chengo Greek skia man'giparang Pluto. Aristotle uamangni
seanirangko an'ching ia somoionaba poraie, uamangni
chanchianirangko uina manenga. Uarangara gamchatgipa hira,
mukta aro sonana bateba gamchatbatsranggipa ong'a."SIKIME"-
