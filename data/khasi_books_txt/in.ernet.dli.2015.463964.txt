KITAP-KO SEGIPANI GIMIN
Ia ' Meghalaya A'dok-o donggipa Garo Hills-ni A'chikrang ingipa
kitap-ko segipa Shri Grohonsing A. Marak ong'a. ua 1925 bilsini Au-
gust jani 31 tariko Goalpara District-ni Budalong minggipa chongipa
songo atchiaha. Uni pagipa-ko Namjang N. Areng aro Magipako Sare
A. Marak mingachim. Songtang Lower Primary School-ko matchote
1940bilsio Nishangram Govt. Aided M.E. School-o poraie, 1945 bilsio
matchotaha. Ua Goalpara P.R. Govt. High School-o 1945-1948 bilsiona
poraie matchotaha. Ua Theological Trainingko, Cherrapunjee Welsh
Mission Theological College-o adita bilsina poraiaha, indiba chusokgipa
ongna manjaha.
Ua 1951 bilsio Miss Billancy Khongwir-ko bia ka'aha. Uamangna
depante sakdokko Isol patiaha. ua bilsi 5-na Kristian Evangelist-ni
kamkoba ka'aha. Ua 1959 bilsioni 1963 bilsiona Bajengdoba Govt. M.E
School-o teacher-ni kamkoba ka'aha. Ua school-ko wate Hills State
Movement-o jak onpaaha. Indake Garo Hills District Council-ni Elec-
tion-o APHLC ticket-o chakate M.D.C. ongaha.
1967 bilsioba Assam Legislative Assembly-na APHLC ticket-ko
ra'e chakate Assam Legislative Assembly-ni MLA-ba ong'aha. 1970-
1971 bilsiona Autonomous Legislative Asembly, Meghalaya-ni M.L.A.
ong'e, Deputy Speaker songako manaha. Ua 1972 bilsio skanggipa
Meghalaya Full Pledged State-ni skanggipa General Election ong'o
chakate M.L.A. ongtaie, Minister-ba ongaha. Ua 1972-1983 bilsiona
Minister ongmitingo South East Asiani dambonga a'songrangko
wenroroe niaha. jekae, Singgapore, Thailand, South Korea, Hongkong
aro Japan. Una agreba Bangladesh-ni capital Dacca songjinmao New
Year-ni salko Capt. W.A. Sangma, Chief Minister baksa manina cholko
man'na baksa Sheikh Mijibur Rehman-ni jikgipaBegum Rehman-koba
grongna cholko manaha. Ua adita kitaprangko A'chik Kusik-chi aro
English-chi seaha. ua 'Meghalaya State-ko Maikai Manaha' ingipa kitap-
ko king 2000 (hajal gni) chapa ka'e uko Pa Purno A. Sangma, Formal
Speaker of Lok Sabha-ni jakchi released 1995 bilsio ka'aha. Aroba
Meghalaya Past and Present-English version aroba Garo Hills and her
people-iarangko chapa kana chol grie dongenga. Ua daoba maiba maiba
kitaprangko sekuenga. Uni bilsi dao 76 ong'aha.
