JANAPANI
Sonatchi aro Kalsinni dakmesokaniko 1937-38 bilsirangoni intalan Achik a-songni salgro
Jolo chong'motan Penchimari, Rongchipatpara, Moskuli, Derek (Assam) aro gipin biaprangoba
dakmesoke mesokaha. Haida, ua sonatchi aro Kalsinni golpoko Bengali oikorchi, Pa Upendro
Ch. Momin sechengaha ine nikaha, 1930 bilsirangon matchotna nangaha.
Kalsin aro Sonatchini ginin chu-ginik uamangni kam aro kattarangni gimin dakmesokani
aganbewalo seaha. Iandakgipa Kalsin aro sonatchini dakmesokani chu'soksrange knalo an'chingni
A'chik bi'sarangna u'i ma'sirike ra'rikchina donangenga maina iandakdipa dakmesokanirang
jatni gisepoda'o gimaangengaha.
Kalsin aro Sonatchini gimin a'sel chong'mot banoni aro nai mai ong-achim u'ina aro ma'sina
dakdilchenggiparango, me'apa chagitchamrango sing-sandie aro sandirikote jeko man'a talatenga.
A'chikrang rokomari dakmcsokanirangko dakmesokna, chrok mesana, git rigng'na, ring'ringna
aro daksningna changgiparang ong'a. Indiba, iarang plakko daksninge dakmesokna chol
ong-pagijaniodongjripna nangaha.
Sonatchi aro Kalsinni daknesokaniko skanggipa dakdilchenggiparangoni Pa Nogen Sangma
jolrangoni ahachengskaha. Ja'manode ka'sine ka'sine gipin biaprangonaba,Jemangan
rokomari nika nianirang A'chik a'songonaba gipbaanichi, 1980 jolrangoni intal ka'sine ka'sine
bonangaha. Ia daknesokanrangara jatni mingsa nambegipa dakbewalko dakmesokani ong'achim.
Haida, ia kitap saoba jatni gisepo iandakgipa dakmesokanirangko ripingangna sikgiparangna
