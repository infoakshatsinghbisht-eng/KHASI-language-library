TALATCHENGANI
Ian angni skanggipa sechenggipa poedo ki-tap ong:a. Poedo
seanirangna angni gisik nangbeaniara ku'tangko nambate man'na jotton
ka'anioni ong-baaha. Ku-tang ong'genchimoba ku-tangni me-suani aro
(Garo) poraigen. Indiba ian namen dal-bea Achik chadamberangn1 gualani
simsakbreja ine nikna man'a. Pagitcham Poedo Segipa Pa. H. D. W. Momin
agana, "Darang do'an ku'tangko knatonikbata ina:" A-chikrangan
de-genma! Iandakgipa ra bianiko gisiktango man-e anga ia chonbegipa poedo
krtapko sena a-bachengaha.
Poedo seanirangko A-chik manderang bree poraina gisik
nangbreja ine ma'sia. Indiba poedo seanirangan bang'a manderangni gisiko
nangebata aro ka'tongo dongkambata. Indake saniba janggi tanganio
nangeanichi seanirangko ning-tue chanchi-bewalanirangkoba naata aro
basakoba uni janggi tanganiko sol-na repna man:pila. Ia segimin poedorang
badita manderangn1 anggi tanganio jakkaltobegipa ong:gen uko anga ma'sina
amja. la segimin katta bichongrang angni janggi tanganirango
chanchianirangko naatbeaha; unigimin poedo seaniko namnikgipa aro
chanchibewalna ranta ka'gipa manderangna gisik nangataniko
on-chongmotgen ine ka-donga. Minggipin, ia chasongni bang-bata A-chik
poedo seanirango gitcham chasongn1 sebewalrango ong-bata ine nika. Aro,
ma-ambin' kattarangko jakkalbrejaha ine nike iandakgipa kattarangko angn1
mitam poedorango ra'gatpile seanirangko dakaha. Ia seanirang
ka-donga.
Ku'mande-Kujaleng ine ia poedo ki-tapko donani
miksonganiara, ku-sikan gamchatbegipa jatrangni warachakani silchidare
aro rama-jalang onga. Ku-sikni gimin jat aro a'song atchia. Ia poedo ki-tapo
adita bilsirangna an'tangni see chimonggimin poedorangoni
mingkolatchisako chapa ka-atna baseaha. A-chikku sea-jotanina on·timinti
on'gilpana chanchian baksa kosako janapgimin miksonganio ia poedo ki-tapko
chapa kaatenga. Ku-ritingko see rakkianichi jatni ku'sikko ripingna aro
ku-sik silroroan baksa Achik jatni sea-jotani kugipinni sea-jotanirangmung
apsan gadango ong-bachina. Angni sika A chik Ku-sik Tangbangchina!

AGANDAPANI
Pilak ku-sikrang aro jatrangon Ku-ritingogipa sea-jotani ba oral
literature donga. Jekon, ku-sikchi aganritinganichi chasongni chasongna
donrikrikanga. Oral Literature-o bang'a rokonni sea-jotanirang gnang,
chong:motan, golpo, rokomantini poedo seanirang, minganirang, poverb,
folk gitrangkoba man'gopa. A-chikkuoba dingtang dingtang rokom
aganritingao ripinggipa sea-jotanirang donga. jekai, katta agana, dani, doro,
ajea, kabe, folk gitrang, golpo, chera ring'ani, aganme-apa, aro uandake.
Iarangan chasongni chasongna pagitchamrangni kuritingchi ripingbagimin
A.chikrangn gam onga.
Americani Baptist missionary-rang skia man'gipa A-chikrangna
Grammar, dictionary, aro primer-rangko A-we ku-sikchi seaha. Jekon,
A-chikku sea-jotanina ra'gatgimin standard ku-sik dake jakkalaha. Sastroko
pe-anio aro mondolioba A-we ku-sikkon jakkalbataha. Uandake, A-chikkuo
pilak sea-jotanirango A-we ku'sikkon jakkale giproroangaha. Indiba
ku-ritingchi ripingbagipa Ku'ritingogipa (oral literature) sea-jotani, sena
jakkalgipa A-we ku'sikoni dingtang ong-a. Iandakgipa rokomni A-chikkuo
sea-jotanirang bon-kamgipa 19-gipa century-ni bakrango bang:roroaha.
Traditional ba pagitchamni sea-jotani aro Lekao Segimin sea-jotani
(Written Literature) gisepo adita dingtanggrikanirang gnang. Traditional
Literature ian aganritingbagimin, uni theme ba katta bichongko A-chik
rang, namnikgipa epic poedorang, aro uamangni u'igipa legend-rang onga.
Minggipinara, lekao segimin, saksa mandechi segimin, segipa mandeni
bimung gnang chapa ka-atgipa ong'a. Aro, lekao segimin sea-jotani 1an
segipa mandeni chanchiatango banaigipa, an'tangni donggimin katta
bichongo, an'tangni serongbewalo, aro jakkalna namnikgipa ku'sik ba
kattako jakkale saksan mandechi segimin onga. Indiba pagitchamni sea-
jotaniara ku-sikchi aganritingbaanio on-rikrikbagipa, uko sawa sechengaha
tangbange rakkigimin, uko jinmaan u-ia, aro uan jat gimikni gam ong'a.
