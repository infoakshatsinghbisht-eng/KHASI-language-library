BRIGIPA CHAPA KA'ATTAIANIO
AGANANI
Ia brigipa chapa ka'attaianio adita ruuta somoiko
ra'na nangahani gimin, poraigiparangna bakbakkale
on'na manjaha ;; maina reanggipa adita bilsirangna
chapa ka'ani lekarangko man'na nenganichi aro chapa
kaani korosrang rang'san tang-doanichi chapa ka'aniko
arbachengatna man'ningjaha.
Iako chapa ka'attaianio anga Garo Hills Book
Emporiumni Shri Sushil Kr. Das-ko mitelna ga aka maina
ua banga nengnikanirang dongoba chapa ka'aniko jako
rae da'o matchotatna man'aha.
Haida ia chapa ka'attainio gualanirang dongode,
ka'sapae angna uiatpaode mitelbeani ong'genchim.

1. SALLINGNI AGANA
BALROROKARI
Salguri mitmat mitmat,
Wa'ambong tiptap tiptap ;
A-sro siao siao,
Salchichaa mitchao mitchao.
A-kinte chadipana sipsap,
Agatek aba tua gekgak,
Aan'ma manggisijok,
Bolnima jakrurongjok.
Wa'an'ma bangsi sike,
A-ko dimu kitbajok ;
Bolnima knal sike
Bolko bijak akbajok.
II. WACHI KARI
A-galmak simnil simil,
Chiabak kimil kimil ;
Sangkira a gil pinjok,
Mikkasim jarim simjok.
A-mong a'du so-ako mikmak,
Chikol an'cheng danako dandak.
Bolchim bite rongrongte dakjok ;
Ang-ke danok parangga bitjok.

SEOKGIMIN POETRYRANG
Dimbil chama chatmiting bakritjak
Bolo, ta'jong wegajok jakripat.
Am'pang agal baliping baljok ;
Taja narot gongriting chajok.
Jongtikket tikket, tikket ;
A-kisang mitek, mitek ;
Chiringo mite achak bekbak ;
Patalo domaski jikjak.
A-skochi doabot tengtang,
Bolgrimo jingjingbeng chengchang.
Bri rike snaru baljok ;
Chiring chisik chiwaring tangjok.
Wachi dilma a kinte golijok ;
Misi a'ma bolganchi suakjok.
Me'a gal-mak pekwatjok.
Angke bisa sa'watjok.
Tebidil dildirajok ;
Ang kesep malpriprajak.
Bima na'tok sambalna dojok,
Bolni bite agatchi minjok.
Mikkasim pripri,
Bolko dodol dokmiting ;
Samsmit mri mri,
Jong-dol nating bitmiting.
III. MISI KARI
Misi a'ma gakuteng ongjok ;
Saksan aba mikkuteng tujok.
Salniango chenggari satjok ;
Rongbirako sepru maljok.
