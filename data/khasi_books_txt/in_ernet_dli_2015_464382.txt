I JINGMUT KA CHAD RWA

I Chad Rwa toh i Chad wada pyrtuit na ki tiar wa daih ha ki 
kjat “Rwa” kiwa pyniatoh juit katkam i rwai ibng ki Nongrwai, i'o’w 
ih i sur wei low iaa-doh-laa phad nadooh ki nongchad hadooh ki 
nongtem, wei wow um mynsien kiwa peit wa bichar.I Chad Rwa 
man i chi bynta ibng ka Chad Sukra, ka la chemlang wow iaiehkmen 
leh shiawbha chwa u hiar rep hiar riang, hajrong ka nongrim wow 
tyoor la kapateih kawaem kajingkitkhlieh, kajingiatylli, i'atoh jingmut, 
wei tule i'ei bha, nadooh na Yung na sem hei longlang long-bi wa ha 
ka Chnong ka Raij kamwa ki Nongrwai, rwai ki, laki sur, wa e sneih 
e kroo, pynibo pynkdaw, wow khooid ka longbru manbru, em akor 
em salon, wei wow tyma u kit wastih kit wakhia la kiwon kiwon ki 
kaam lada iwa khian wadei, nae waheh wa khroo.

Haka “Chad Rwa” Ye u Ya chim bynta da kiwon kiwon ki 
kynthai, nadooh ki khynnah, Khyllood, hadooh ki man Bei katkam 
wadawa i sur rwai nae jingmut i surwa batai Ya ki kaam, ki jingthmu, 
lada Yeiwa mong u syrpood, Yeiwa tiiaw u pynka-it, Yeiwa dukha u 
lana, Yeiwa swait u pynchngaih. Yei lymboit u pynchiap wa Yeiwa Jcat 
u syrdep. Ha kawa ki nongtem nongput waki nongrwai kynthai wa 
chynrang pynwandur ki deiwa Yalai lang i kieh “Ki Rwa” ha ki kjat 
Yong ki nongchad, kiwa pynkhih Ya ki kti, ki kjat wow batai Ya i jingmut 
Yong ki kyntien wa rwai u nongrwai. Boonsien ki rwai Yong “Ka 
Chad Rwa” toh kiwa hikai wow hiawkor Ya ki jingkyrkhu wada Yoh 
nadooh ki Ryngkaw ki Samon, ki Loom ki Wah, ki I'hwai ki La- 
ngen, ki samoi wa kiwa em soodong hei jingim bru, khamtam wei e 
mynsien Ya ka Kchu ka siang wow syndong Yei Yung Yei sem Yei jinglong 
khooid longhok , wym hoi u im laitlooid laitlan, u im lyngkar. heipor
