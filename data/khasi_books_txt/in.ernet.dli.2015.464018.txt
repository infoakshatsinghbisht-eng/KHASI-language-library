Ia golpo mingkolgrik anga an'tangni seanirang ongja,
pe'skagiminrangsa. Golporangko dingtang dingtang
ki-taprangoniko ra'aha. Iarangoniko bang-batakon Nokdangni
Ripeng aro Do.kua magazinerango gatmanaha. Golpo
minggittamde, chong'motan, “Alda Noko Golmal," "Good Night
Ma Goodbye" aro "1946-ni Christmas," ong'chongmotgipa
"Ja-ade A -stenisa" ia minggnikode kan'dikgipa golporangni ning'o
napatna man:ja.
"Arthurni Siani"-ko “"King Arthur And The Knights of the
Round Table"-oniko raa, jekon “Morte D' Arthur" ineba minga.
Golpoko segipa Sir Thomas Malory (1430-1471) uni ja-manchi
janggi tanganio an'chi-pari nikate golmal gondogol ong'ate janggi
tangahani gimin, uko baditaba bilsirangna pattoko donataha.
Pattoko dongmitingo Malory ia golpoko seaha. Uni siani bilsi 14-
ka-chenggipa William Caxton ia ki-tapko chappa ka'ataha. “Jaade
A-stenisa"-ra Americani Presidentrangni janggi tanganiming
nangchapgipa obostarang ong-a. Americani (USA) Presidentrang
dal-dalgipa ningsinggipa manderang ong'a, indiba ramram
manderangni kusi ong'e, duk ong'e aro basakoba ka-ding wacheke
Ramram manderangni gunrang uamangoba donga, maina gisik
ka'tongode uamangba ramram manderangsan-ja'ade a'stenisan.
"Ja-ade A-stenisa"-o 1789-1981 bilsirangni gisepo President
ong'giparangni sakprakni janggi tanganioniko mingprak
obostakosan onaia.

Banobara orto apsansranggipa A-chikku kattako man'jae orto

Kan-dikgipa golporang mingpraknin dingtangmancha
ong'ani donga aro pe-skaanio uko jaktuatna man'ja. Pe-skagipa
kattani ba sentenceni ortoko ku'prete ku'gipino seskaanio
sechongmotani bimang, bewal aro gunrangoni agre chel-angna
nangja.

AGANDAPANI
Dingtang ku-sikoniko A-chikkuona pe-skaaniara rakbegipa
kam ong'a. Sakgipinni segiminkosa pe-skaengani gimin, uko tiktak
kattarangko on'e ma'siatna namen neng beani ong-a; maina kattako
namnika kattarangko donatoara inangsongaoni sretnaba donga. Ma.
Brucellish K. Sangma nambegipa, knatobegipa, kragipa kattarangko
jakkale ia ki-tapo ming kolgrik golporangko pe'skaaha. Ua
kingsamangmang ki-taponiko ragija, bang-a dingtang dingtang
ki-taprangoniko ra'e pe-skagiminko kadime ki-tap kingsana tariaha.
