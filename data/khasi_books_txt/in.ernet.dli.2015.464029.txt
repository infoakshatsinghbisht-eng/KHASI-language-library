AGANSOCHENGANI
A-gilsakni niksenggipa ku-sikrangchi sea-jotanirangko A-chikkuchi
segiminrang baksa tosusaate nigenchimode banggija bilsirangonisa sena
a hachengaha ine nikgen. Da-alona dipet bang-gija ki-taprangkosan chapa
ka-engpitti. A-chikkuo bang'a dingtang dingtang miksonganirango,
manderangni nangnikengggipa aro nichakenggipa seanirango ki-taprang
dongchongmotna nangengaha. An-chingna skang, English Missionary-rang
aro A.chik pagitcham-ma gitchamrang an·chingna kasabee ki-taprangko see
donangaha. An'ching da'ororoni chadanberang uamangko mittelna
nangchongmota. A.chikkuo seanirangna ontisa changa-sapaniko
on'gilpaengon ian tingtotsa chi gita ong'angpachina ine anga ia ki-tapko
sece nienga indiba ki-taprangko bang'atnamangmang seaiode uarang
jakkaltogipa ong'skajawachin; gisikna namgipa cha-aniko ongipa
ki ldprangkosa an·chingna nanga.
Ki-tap segipa mande seng-begipa, uibegipa aro ki-tap seatangni
ku-sikko jojrangpile ma-sigipa ong-chongmoina nanga; ian niam ine anga
nikmanaha. Unbaksana, Pa. Hubert K Sangmani "Niksenggipa
mikronrungna ugre sukgipinni nana gunko nikuu altuu ong ju" ingipa
agananikoba gisik ra'ate, an'tango chu'onggijanirangko anga
nikatmanengachim. Indonga chanchianirangchi dongbokengon, golpoko
porainan'gipa ripengrang iako ki-tapni himangona ra-bachina angna
didianiko on-aha. Ma. Viola Sonatchi B. Sangma (Upper Chandmari, Tura)
ia golpoko che-en che-em poraic nic gamchatbegipa namdapatanirangko
dakaha aro ku'pattibeaha.
"Mangsunduk Tarigipu Paningsa" ia songadamni janggi
tangbewalo pangchake segipa golpoko ki-tapni binango ong-katatna
man'ahanina Nokgipa Isolko mittela. Ma. Viola Sonatchi B. Sangmani

AGANDAPANI
'Mangsunduk Tarigipa Paningsa' lan ku-akmao golpo
seani bimang onga. Ia ki-tapko segipa, Wilberth D. Marak, kangalgipa
tanggiparang maikai Kristian toromona ka-sinbee an-pilaha aro maikai
dingtangggipa janggi tangbewalko chagrongahachim, iani gimin
rongtale janapaha.
Songadamo mandeni songsaro janggi tangani bewal
(cha-bewal, robewal..) aro uni ramramgipa bebe ra'anirang, uianirang,
kenanirang aro uni songsul-noksulrang baksa duk suko bakrimanirang
-uarangko janapanirangko nikna man'a.
Poraisoenggipani gisikko an-saoatna, obosta ong'anirango,
agangrikao aro talatanirangko dakanio katta jakkalanirangchi
ka-dingatani cholrangko jakkalaha.
larang baksana, pante-me-tra ka-sagrik-mikchagrikani
obostarangkoba on-chapjolaha.
Indake ua mandeni salanti janggi tanganio ga'chate, uni
miksonge parakatna skani, mande toromo kakket ong'e beberae
tangode, neng begipa aro nangbemiting sal somoio dingtang dingtang
tangani gadangoni salchroako man'e, chukalgipa kusi ong:gipa tangani
gadang aro salrangna ka-dongsoani dongchongmota ine ong-a.
Wilberth D. Marak, ia ki-tapna agreba bang-a golporangko
ong:a. Uni joton ka bee seani kamrang gipin manderangni gisikkoba
sena-jotna gita didiani ongchina.
Bang-bata aro namnambata seanirang uni kolomoni
ong-katdapkugen ine ka-dongsoani gnang aro ua, uarango chu-sokgipa
ong'e, A-chikni sea-jotani a-bao ongilaniko dakangchina angni
kakketgipa skanirang uni kamrango aro uni janggi tanganini
chu-sokgnina ong-china.
V.S.B.Sangma
Upper Chandmary, Tura.

FOREWORD
I happen to read the English translation of
'MANGSUNDUK TARIGIPA PANINGSA', a maiden attempt
of folktale writer, Shri Wilberth D. Marak, a young and promising
short story writer in Garo, and found that through this folktale, the
writer is trying to express his mind as if it is a real happening in life.
I am convinced that those who happen to read this folktale
will definitely find it interesting and will capture them into the realm
of folklore.
Dated Shillong.
