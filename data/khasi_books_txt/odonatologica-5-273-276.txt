REMARKS
Calicnemia mukherjeei
sp. n. is very
close
to C. miniata (Selys, 1886), but is
larger in size,
with the ground
colour paler red, and the
nodal index a little
higher.
The two species
also differ in some details in the structure of the penis.
So far
as is known, C. mukherjeei sp. n. is restricted to around Shillong in the
altitudinal ranges
1400-1700 meters. The males were
collected at the foot of a
