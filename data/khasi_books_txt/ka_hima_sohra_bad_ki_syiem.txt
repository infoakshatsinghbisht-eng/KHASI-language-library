KA HIMA SOHRA BAD KI SYIEM HYNDAI
Da U Hormu Rai Diengdoh (1888)

Ka Bynta I: Ka Sdang jong ka Hima Sohra
Ka Hima Sohra ka long kawei na ki Hima ba rim tam bad ba khraw bor tam ha Ri Khasi. Na ki spah snem hyndai, ki Syiem Sohra ki la synshar ia ka ri da ka burom, ka hok, bad ka jingiatreilang bad ki Myntri (Ki Khadar Kur).

Ka Durbar jong ka Hima:
U Syiem um lah ban leh katba mon; u dei ban pyniaid ia ka kam ryngkat bad ka Durbar Hima. Lada ka Durbar kam mynjur, ym don hukum ba lah ban trei kam. Kane ka pyni ba ka rukom synshar Khasi ka long kaba beit, kaba khuid, bad kaba pyniasoh ia u paidbah bad ka hok.

Ka Bynta II: Ki Mawbyrshem bad Ka Jingiada ia ka Ri
Ha ki por thma, ki rangbah shlur jong ka Hima Sohra ki la ieng ban iada ia ki khap ri. Ki la shna ia ki mawbyrshem, ki kut-maw, bad ki jaka kiew thma kiba la pynneh ia ka burom jong ka jaitbynriew.

Ka Jingwan jong ki Phareng:
Ynda ki Phareng ki la wan sha Sohra ha ka snem 1829, ki Syiem Sohra ki la iateh ktien ban pynneh ia ka bor synshar lajong halor ki khyndew ki shyiap bad ki kur ki jait. Ka jingthoh halor ki mawlynnai ka ieng sah haduh mynta ban sakhi ia ka burom jong ka Ri Khasi.
