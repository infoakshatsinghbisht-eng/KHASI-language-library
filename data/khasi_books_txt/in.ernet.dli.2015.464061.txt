ang'keba dongja aro rong'terangoba gitchak gitchak dake

chakgreine mingachim.
Salde napmanahachim. Panterangni aumiksuna re'-
baako grongdikna kene Na'bat skelbakel aubroke re'-
bapilengachim. Ua ramao pante jinmako jikjakroroe re'-

ua uina man'achim. Uamang Na'batna jrip jrip rama

 batde-gisiktango tikkit tikkit chanchiaha.
Ma'a paa senggaang meatam cha'e barandao neng'-
taksoengachim. Ma'gipa wa'sco asonge me'rakku bikapo
sada dol'e ringengachim. Pagipara morao ja'a ra.dopile
asongesa, pakmao dan'dane suk ong'e mola opengachim.
Na'bat gansre chinsremane mi cha'na babilsichi napang-
aha. Chudapo wa'al so'chimitako spoe ua chakki so'aha.
Salgimiko ong'a a'selrang, uamangni ka'dinge agane

okamataha. Ma'gipa pagipani gisepo sawa mai kattako
