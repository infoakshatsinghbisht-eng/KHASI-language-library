(h) KA REP KA RIANG BAD KA JINGKYLLA KA
RUKOM REP :
Ha ki spah bad ki phew snem kiba la leit noh, ka jingtrei
kaba kongsan tam ha Ri Khasi Jaifitia ka long ka rep. Ha kitei Id
snem kim pat don lane kim pat sngewthuh ban trei ia kiwei pat Id
jait kam. Ki don kiba trei ia kiwei pat ki jait kam hynrei kiba bun
ki im da ka rep ka riang. Ha ki kitei ki snem, ka khyndew ka long
kaba sbur bad seisoh bha bad ki briew Id lah ban im pahuh pahai
tang da ka kam rep wat la kim don kiwei pat Id jait jingtrei. Lah
ban thung bun ki jait jynthung kynthup ia u soh u pai bad Id jhur
ki jhep laiphew jait. Ki jingrep kiba kham kongsan ki long u kba,
u riewhadem, krai, u phan myngngor bad phan karo, ka shriew,
u phan dieng bad bun jait ki jait jhur. Ha ki thaifl ri War bad ri
Bhoi, ki jynthung bakham kongsan ki long u kba, u kwai, u
tympew, u soh niamtra, u soh mrit khlaw, ka shriew bad kiwei
kiwei. Ha ki thaifl Jaifltia, u kba, u soh niamtra bad bun kiwei pat
ki jait jhur. Ha ki thaifl pdeng ka ri Khasi, u phan karo, u phan

myngngor, phan dieng, kba, u riew hadem, u krai bad ki jait jhur
lai phewjait. Ha ki thaifl Bhoi bad Jaifitia, ka jur bha ka rep kba
bad ka biang ban pyndap ia ka jingdonkam ha ri Khasi Jaifitia. U
synsar uba ju long mynno mynno u mar khlaw, u kylla long mynta
uwei na ki jynthung bakongsan uba bsa ia ki nongrep khamtam ha
ki thaifl ri War bad ri Bhoi.
Ka rukom rep :
Ha ri Khasi Jaifltia ka rukom rep bad ka jingpyndonkam ia u
symbai ka long ha kajuh ka rukom ha ka ri baroh kawei lait noh
ha ki katto katne ki thaifl, kum ka thaifl Bhoi, Lyngngam bad
Jaifltia ha ki jaka ba dang rben ka khlaw ki pyndonkam ka rep
jhum bad shyrti, ha ki thaifl War ki pyndonkam ki rep bri ne
bsah, bad ha ki jaka pdeng ka ri Khasi Jaifltia ki pyndonkam bun
rukom Id jait jingrep.
1. Ka rep Jhum : Ka rep jhum ka long kawei na ki rukom rep
kaba pynsyrwa ia ka khlaw, kaba pynsynlen hi ki lum Id
them bad kaba pynkylla dew rkhiang ia ki jaka ba seisoh.
Haba rep jhum donkam ban pom pynngat ne pynkyllon
na phang ia ki dieng ki siej ha khyndew naduh kiba rit tam
haduh ki diengbah. Ka shim por slem slem ban iap bad

tyrkhong kine ki dieng. Nangta sa thang da ka ding haduh
ban da kylla dpei. Ynda la dei ka aiom bet, sa puh bad kynroi
ia ka dew dpei bad sa thung jynthung ne sa bet !a u symbai.
Ka rep jhum ka long tang ha ki jaka ba rben bha ka khlaw
bad ka shim por da ki phew snem ban pynlong khlaw pat.
Shisien ba la rep jhum kato ka jaka ka sah shrah bad synlen
da ki phew phew snem. Ha kane ka juk mynta ka rep jhum ka
la nangduh nangduh wat haduh ki thaifi ba kyndong tam jong
ka ri Khasi Jaifltia na ka daw bym don khlaw shuh kaba biang
ban rep jhum. Ka rep jhum ka kham seisoh bun bun shah ban
ia kiwei pat ki rukom rep tangba ka long tang shisien thung
ne shisien pynhap symbai bad nangta lada rep biang ha kajuh
ka jaka kam seisoh shuh kumba mynshwa namar ka dpei kaba
mih na kaba thang ia ka khlaw ka la kylla khyndew noh bad
ka biang tang shu rep syllai.
2. Ka rep Shyrti : Ka rep shyrti ka long kumjuh kum ka rep
jhum. Ka jingiapher ka long ba ha ka rep jhum donkam ban
pom pynkyllon naphang ia ki dieng naduh kiba rit haduh ki
diengbah katba ha ka rep shyrti ki pynsah teng ia ki dieng

bah ne ki dieng heh ha kata ka rukom ba kito ki dieng kiba
sah kin kha symbai pat ban pynlong khlaw biang. Ka rukom
rep bad ki symbai ki long kumjuh kum ka rep jhum. Ha ka
rep jhum la shu thang pathar ia ka khlaw katba bam ka ding
katba ha ka rep shyrti ka don da ka pud ban thang ia ka
khlaw. Ka shyrti khlaw ka long kawei pat ka rukom thang
hi ka khlaw ban iada na ka jingpur ding sha kawei pat ka
khlaw.
3. Ka rep Bun : Ka rep bun ka long kawei pat ka rukom rep ha
kaba thang la ka khlaw. Ka iapher shibun na ka rep jhum ne
rep shyrti. Ha ka rep bun, kano kano ka jaka ne lum kaba
long khlaw lah ban rep bun - ym da donkam ka khlaw kaba
rben. Haba rep bun donkam ban prat lut ia u phlang, ki dieng
ki siej bad ki niut ki flier kiba don ka khlaw bad sharud khlaw
ruh. fa ki dieng kiba rit dei ban pynkyllon lut baroh bad i'a ki
dieng kiba kham heh la ju ieh noh bad tyngkai - ym baroh.
Borabor, i'a ki dieng kiba heh la shu ngor lane mait tang ia ki
tnat bad ieh noh ia ki tnat kliar khnang ba ki dieng kin im bad

kin bet symbai pat ynda la dep rep bun.
Ynda la dep prat ne khet ia ka khlaw baroh kawei kat kum ka
jingdonkam ban rep, la ju ieh noh katto katne ta'iew ba ka
sngi kan shat bad ba ki dieng ki siej ne u phlang kin rkhiang
bad kin iap noh. Ynda ki la sdang ban kem saw bad rkhiang
ne lam tyrkhong bad iap, ka la dei ka por ban lum ia ki
lynnong lynnong ban pynlong nur (bed seed). Borabor, ia ka
nur la ju shna ha ka dur kum ka tusok thiah kaba ka jingheh
ka long kumba lai phut, ka jingrben kumba shi phut bad ka
lynter ka long borabor hapdeng phra bad shi phew phut. Halor
ka nur la tap da u stong phlang uba stang bad halor u stong
pat la ju tap da ka khyndew met kaba rkhiang bad hadien
haba u stong bad ka khyndew ki la tyrkhong bad rkhiang bha
sa thang da ka ding haduh ban da long dpei. Ia une u dpei la
khot ka bun ne u 'bun . Ha ki phew snem kiba la leit noh,
bunsien, halor u bun la ju thung da u phan myngngor bad
phan karo, nangta sa pynbud ban thung da u jhur lai phewjait.
Na ki rukom rep baroh, ka rep bun ka long kaba siesoh shibun

tangba ka long ka kam kaba sani bad lut ksan palat.
4. Ka rep Khylliap ne rep Khlip : Ka rep khylliap ka long
kawei na ki rukom rep bun kaba duna ka jinglut hynrei kaba
shitom haduh katta katta ha ka rukom trei. Ban rep khylliap
donkam ban ioh ka jaka kaba madan, kaba long ryntih, kaba
long dewmet bad kaba long ryntih u langsoh (phlangsoh),
kam pher Ia ka long ka jaka kaba heh ne kaba rit, ka long kat
lnim kajingdonkam . Ka jaka kaba khleh khyndew bad shyiap,
kaba long dieng, kaba khohruh khohram bad kaba don ki
thied dieng, kaba long tyrkhang bad kiwei ki kynja fluit kiba
kyllaifl bad suh thied kam long satia ban rep khylliap ne khlip.
Ki mohkhiew ki dei ban long kiba nep, kiba stang bad kiba
heh. Tang ki briew kiba tbit bad mlien i'a kane ka rukom rep
ki lah ban leh bad kam long ka rukom rep kaba lah ban i'a rep
paidbah, nalor kata, ka long ruh kaba shitom ban ioh ka jaka
kaba madan khamtam ha kane ka ri lum jong ngi.
Shwa ban rep, donkam ban pynkhuid i'a kata ka jaka kaba
mut ban rep bad dei ban till tang ia ki langsoh kiba soh ha ka
dewmet. i'a kane ka rukom rep la ju sdang borabor naduh u

donkam. Kane ka rukom rep ka long kaba shitom bad bniah
bha hynrei kaba duna pat ka jingpynlut ha ka rukom trei.
5. Ka rep Syllai : Ka syllai ka thew la ka khyndew ha ka jaka
lum kaba la dep rep bun - kaba dang shu rep bun shen lane
kaba la dep rep bun la kham stem. Namar ka syllai ka long ka
khyndew kaba la dep rep bun kumta ka rukom trei ka long
kaba kham suk. Haba rep syllai donkam ban tih lane kynroi
ia ka khyndew shalor ba kan pra bad jem. Ynda la dep tih
iehnoh katto katne por ia ka khyndew ba kan 'ioh sngi bad
Iyer. Ha ka por ba thung ia u phan donkam ban ber katto
katne ka sboh masi lane kano kano ka sboh bad sa bynrap ban
ber khyndiat ka sboh dawai. Ha ka por mynshwa ba dang
sbur ka khyndew, ym ju pyndonkam ia ka sboh dawai. Ia u
phan uba thung ha ka khyndew dei ban ieh tang hapdeng
khyndai phew bad shispah san sngi bad sa khlaw noh. Lada
ieh slem palat ia ka por, u phan u lah ban pyut noh. Ka

jingtrei ha ka rep syllai ka kham suk shibun bad ka jingmih u
phan ruh ka duna shibun ban ia ka rep bun. Kumba long ka
rep bun, ka rep syllai ruh lah ban thung ban jait ki jait jhur,
kum u kubi, u muli bad bun kiwei pat ki jait jhur. Lah ruh
ban rep kba uba ju khot u ba-syllai tangba ka jingmih ka
duna shibun na u kba-pynthor ne u kba-bun.
