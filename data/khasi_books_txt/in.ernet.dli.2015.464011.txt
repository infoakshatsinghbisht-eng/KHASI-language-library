(i)
AGANCHENGANI
la ki-tapko seon anga bakgni dake sualaha. Skanggipa
bakara "Katta Jikse ba Kujikserang" aro gnigipa "Poedorang"
ong:a. Kujikse aro Poedorangko seengon, anga darangni segimin
ki-tap ba kattarangoniko ra'gate seade dongja, indiba badiabarango
apsan kattarangko semangenchimode uarangna kemako bi-enga.
Ku jikserangko seanio badiaba kattarangde apsan melijanaba
gnang, maina angni atchiram jolo naikai aganrongachim, uarango
pangchakesa seaha.
la ki-tapko seanio chu-sokna dakchakahanina Nokgipa
Isolko mitelchenga aro tangka-paisako on'e dakchakahanina Di-
rector of Art and Culturc Meghalaya, Shillong-ko mitelanbaksa
Williamnagar District Librarian aro Staff-ko mitela; maina
uamangba segrik-jotgrikanio angko dakchakbeaha.
Dingtangmancha Pa Rickas D. Shirako mitela maina ia Ki-tapko
ong-atna, banan(spelling)rangko tik onge jakkalna mesoke aro
kurpattie on-aha. Pa Shan D. Sangma koba mitela maina uaba,
pilak tarina nanganirangko tarian baksa, bakrangko donjujae
on'anio dakchakaha.Una agreba angni jikgipa, Ma N. D. Shira-
koba dingtangnancha mitela, maina uaba katta sandianirango angko
dakchakbeaha.
Sena-jotna changpajaoba, je skanirangko Isol angna
On-aha, uarangko jakkalenba ia ki-tapko seaha. Haida ian da ororo
poraienggipa chadamberangna adita dakchakani ong:chongmotgen
ine anga kadonga. la ki-tapko anga, sianggimin angni pagipa.,
Nelson A. Sangma aro magipa, Tolmoni B.Marak -na on-kangan
(dedicate ka-an) baksa atchiram songtang: Ganolgre-ko gisik
ra-kame dongna ine seaha. Ki-tapko seanio banoba gualanirang
On-anina mitelbegen.

(iv)
AGANDAPANI:
Salantio A-chik ku-sikko agananio aro sea jotanio badita
ga-kime agana aro sena-jotna man'a inditan su-ua aro knatoa.
Indake aganna gita an-ching noko aroba songsalo salantio agangipa
kattarang baksana nitoe katta jikserangko jakkaina manode uan
knatobatgipa ong'a ine nikna man'a. Unigiminan daororo.
dingtangdingtang poraiani gadangrango katta kujikserangko
rachapaha.
Wilnen B. Marak,(Cheangkalpa) ia nanganiko nike
dingchikbee kattarangko sandi kolame 'Kujikse ba Katta-
Jikserang aro Poedorang' ine katta bichongko done ku:jikse aro
adita poedorangko see chapa ka-ataha.
A.chik chadamberang jatni nangipa dakbewal aro maniani
bewalrangna simsakgijanichi uarang ong siroroangengaha. Ia ong'a
obostarangko nike, Kitapko segipa salantio manderangni jenggi
tanganiko namdapatna ba de-mesanagita adita poedorangkoba
seaha. Uni ia namgipa kam jatni namgipa hewalko tangchapilna
gita poraigipa sakantina dakchakbeani ong'gen ine ka donga.
