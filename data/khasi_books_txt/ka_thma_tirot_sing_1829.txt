KA THMA U TIROT SING PYRSHAH IA KI PHARENG (1829-1833)
Da Dr. Hamlet Bareh (1954)

Ka Bynta I: Ka Jingieng Rasong jong U Tirot Sing
Ha u bnai Iaiong 1829, ynda u Syiem Tirot Sing u la iohi ba ki Phareng ki thmu ban shna surok ban klun ia ka bor synshar jong ki Khasi, u la khot ia ka Durbar Bah ha Nongkhlaw.

Ka Hukum ka Durbar:
Ka Durbar ka la rai da kawei ka sur ban kyntait ia ka jingshah thngan bad ban iada ia ka ri haduh ka drop snam kaba khatduh. Ki khnam, ki ryntieh, bad ki waitlam ki la sawa kylleng ki lum bad ki them.

Ka Bynta II: Ka Thma ha ki Kram bad Gorges
Da ki snem, u Tirot Sing bad ki rangbah shlur kum u Monbhut bad u Lorshai ki la pynjop ia ki shipai Phareng da ka thma kynsan (Guerrilla Warfare). Ki kram maw bad ki khlaw rben ki la long ki kut ba khlain.

Ka Ktien ba Sah Khatduh:
Haba la kem ia u da ka buit-tuh, ki Phareng ki la kular ban pynlong syiem pat ia u lada u siew khajana. Hynrei u Tirot Sing u la ieng da ka burom bad u ong: 'Kham bha ban iap kum u syiem ba laitluid ban ia kaba im kum u syiem mraw ha u Phareng!' U la khlad noh ha phatok Dhaka ha u 1835 kum u kpa jong ka jinglaitluid.
