AGANCHENGANI
A·chik Literature Societyko a.bachengao Garo Literature Society ine
biming donachim. Ja'mano Societyni Executive Committee tom'aniko
dakate, chanchirime, "Garo" kattani pal "A·chik" kattako on'skana tik
ka'aha. Indake Societyko "A-chik Literature Society" ine mingskaaha.
A.chik Literature Society A-chikku Sea-Jotaniko nambatatna, bariatna,
gadang tangdoatna aro am-kolamna jotton ka-enga. A-chik Bananko tik
ka-na gita, oikor mingantina Sub-Committeerangko done, kattarangni
bananko tik ka'ani kamko a-bachengengaha. Somoi ra-gen; indiba salsaode
an.ching sakantian an·tangtangni namnika ba ongnika banankon
jakkalaienga. Bano ‘t' ba “k' ge-gni ong'gen, ‘raka' dongama dongja,
namatna Society kam ka'enga.
MBOSE-ni A-chikkuo Class-Ioni XII-ona tarigipa ki-taprangko,
subjectantinan che-eme niaha. Classni kri text-ni melia meligijani, katta
bichongrangko baseani, mingantikon, subjectna kragipa manderangko Sub-
Committee done sandiaha. Ia Sub-Committeerangni am'e
mangiminrangko dondime, Report tarie, Reportko MBOSE-ni Executive
Chairman-na on-aha. Indiba da'alonaba uamangni aganchakaniko
man·kuja.
Bilsiantian A.chik Literature Society General Conferenceko
ong'atronga. Ia Conferenceo A-chik Literature Societyni Niam Amako
ba Constitutionko namdapatna nangnikode namdapata,
chanchirimanirangko daka aro Seminar ong-ate, Lekkarangko sebaate,
uarangnibidingo chanchianirangkodaka.
A.chik Literature Society jagittamprako “A-chik Ku'rang"
magazineko chapa ka-ata. Man-adipet, A-chik jatni songsar, jatni dakbewal-
rikbewalrang, niam-bewalrang, niam-retirang, A.chik Ku-sik, magitcham
