1.KI PLANET
KUMNO BAN ITHUH IA KI
Ha ka Kitab kaba Ar, ngi la .pule shaphang
ka kynhun planet jong ngi bad ia ki jingjngai
jong uwei uwei u planet na ka Sngi. Ha kane ka
lynnong ngin pyrshang ban kdew kumno ngi lah
ban ithuh lada ngi peit ia ki ha sahit bneng ha
ka miet ba shai bha ka bneng.
U planet uba jan eh ia ka Sngi u dei u Mercury.
U long ruh ,uba rit tam na ki planet baroh.
Ngim ju iohi koit ia u namar u ia bud ryngkat
ryngkat bad ka Sngi. Ngi lah ban iohi ia u
tengkhat, hadien ba ka Sngi ka la sep ne shuwa
ba kan mih.
U Venus u kham jngai ban ia u Mercury na
ka Sngi. Une u planet u dei u met bneng uba
phyrnai bad uba itynnat tam ha sahit bneng.
Ngi lah ban iohi ia u kum u 'Lur Step shaphang
mihngi ha ka por kynjat shai bad kum u' 'Lur
Miet shaphang sepngi hadien ba la sep ka Sngi.
U Venus u heh lajan kat ka pyrthei hi.
U planet uba lai dei ka Pyrthei jong ngi.
Hadien ka Pyrthei bid sa u Mars. Une.. u Mars
u kham rit khyndiat ia_ ka Pyrthei., Ia une u
planet ki ju khot ruh u Planet Meh Ding, namar
ka jingshai jong u ka lam byrsaw, kum ka, ding.
Lada ngin peit ia u da ki dorbin kiba khlai
ngin iohi ba don ki dak ha u, kiba lieh bad kiba
iong. Ngim pat lah ban ong thikna kine ki dei
kiei. Don kiba tharai ba don briew kiba shong
ha une u planet, hynrei ia kane ruh ngim pat lah
pynshisha.

U Jupiter u long u planet uba phyrnai bha
lait noh na u Venus. U'heh khatlai shah ia ka
Pyrthei bad u don 8 tylli ki bnai kiba shad
tawiar ia u. Don katto katne ki lyoh ha u, bad
lyngba ki dorbin.
U planet ubai phylla eh u dei u Saturn. Ym
don uwei pat u met bneng kum ma u. Lyngba
ki dorbin ngi lah ban iohiia u ba u lam shaltuin
khyndiat. Sawdong jong u pat don lai ban ki
ksah (rings) kiba ker tawiar ia u. Ka long kaba
iphylia kumno kine ki ksah ki neh hi ha suin
khlem jingkyrshan eiei na u planet. Kine ki lah
ban long tang ki lak bad ki phew lak ki bnai rit
kiba shad sawdong ia u Saturn.
U Uranus u shad 10 kynta 45 minit ha la u
jong u peiba (diameter) bad u, shim por 84 snem
ban tawiar ia ka Sngi.
U Neptune u shad
sawdong ia lade kumba 8 kynta bad u iaid tawiar
ia ka Sngi shisien ha ka 165 snem. U Pluto pat
u shim por haduh 248 snem ban iaid tawiar
ja ka Sngi namar ba u long uba jngai tam na
ki planet baroh.
Ka long kaba kham shitom ban lap ia ka
jaka ki planet --- khamtam naduh u Mars
shaneng --ha ka sahit bneng shisien wad da ka
khmat. Ki, iing-peit-khlur (observatory) ki ju
bneng man, ki por. 'Da kaba peit ia kine ki
map, ngin kham, suk ban lap ia ki khlur ne
planet ba ngi kwah ban iohi.
