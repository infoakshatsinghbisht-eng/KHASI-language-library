Agandapani
Anga Achik ongeba A-chik A-songni buring bolgrimrangni bidingo, a'dokni matburingrangni aro
do-orangni gimin u-ianio ja manchakbea. Ila angni bilgrianiko u-ieba, Pa Llewellyn R. Marak Pangrakpa, uni
tarigiminko name poraie nion, rokomari chanchianirang, gisik raatpilanirang aro gisikni re-ani a'palona
nakatna seksektokaha. Do-o matrang A-chikrangni janggi tanganio chapchapbate donggiparang ong'a.
A.chik A songni nitoaniko janapode, do-o matrangni bang'ani aro rokomari ong aniko janapatgijan ong ja.
Skangtalan do-o matrang A.chikrangni janggi tanganio bilmanbea; aganritinganirango, golporango aro katta
A chik A-songo do-orang bang:beoba aro an'chingni janggi tanganio uarang mongsonggipa bakko
raoba, uarangni jat-ma'mal, dongani cha'ani, bima bipa joraani, bitip dakani aro uarang gita bidingrango
am'e sandie niani aro see donanirang dongja. Do-onamangmang gisik on'aigipa manderang an'chingni gisepo
nakatkuja. Gipin a'songrango do-orangko ja-rikbite,jako derbin ra'e dongramtango donggipa do-orangko
ma'mal sandie nigiparang ba bird watcher-rang donga. Bird-bath ba auchakaniko, bird-houseko ba bitchi
chichakaniko aro bird-tableko ba cha'ani gatchake dongipako rike ona. Do-orangni cha'gipa bite-
bitchilrangko katepe pala. Chinaode do-rong bitipo angipa atta dakgipaoniko soup tarigipakon mingsingpila.
Sandie nigiparang bol gadoe bitipni bidingo bitchini bidingo u-idape ra'na jotton ka'a. Badiaba
adoko nikronggipa do-orangko nikangjahaon, duk onga; bilsigipino bilbataiako nikon, bi-sa atchiana kusi
ong'a gita gisik katchatoka.
Ku'minchi senokgiparang bird-song ba bird-call, chong'motan do-o mikoaoniko aro uni bewaloniko
 an'saoataniko nisengsoa. Mandena ka-dongsoani, an'saoani aro gisik an'senganiko on'gipa ia do-orang
an'ching gisiko u-ina skanirangkoba nakatata. Badia do-orang sin-kario bachina apjitanga? Sagalko batsoto
nengjama aro ramako maidake u-ia? Maiko cha'e knatobee ring'na changa? Ring'ani apsanaiora,
sandidape ra'ode gimikan nambegen.
Ia ki-tapo, Korokni do-o rokomprakko goe, chimonge, New Yorkni National Museumona watatna
tarianiko janapa. Ia chasongode do-o-rangko goe siatna onjajok., Dikdiksana jadipchangatgipa samrangko
jakkale, a'doktang - dongramatangon do-orangni bidingo sandianirangkosa da'ororo daka. Indiba Korokni
do-orangko jarike, dongani - cha'ani aro gipin bewalrangko sandie nianiara ramram kam ongja.
Korokni sandie see rakkianiko aro ia ki-tapo seaniko poraie, anga chonmitingni salrangko
nikjagringatpila. Attamo do-bret bilraprapa, sarao do-kru saru jrakjrak, songreo dogrik do-mesal katsuseta;
do-amek mikoode sokgipana nichaka; do-uang mikoode an'tesrenga. Do-de alabokni aro Dodikeni gitko
do-orangni bimingrangmangmangba saksano chanchie ka dingsimitata. Indiba, da ode maidakesa uarangni
mikoarangko knajaengjok ine chanchibaon, katchani dimelangpila.

Skang chasongode do-deba dongachim ine Korok aro ia ki-tapko segipa janapa. Ia do-orangni
gimaangenganiara jajrenganiko ra-baa. Do-o mat donggijagipa a:gilsakko chanchiate nibo. Walni andalanian
chotchanggja gita, srik ritik gopramo donga gitasa, a-gilsako janggitanganide ong aignok.,A gilsaktangko
ia obostaona sokatna sikjaode, an-ching do-o matrangko ripingna rikna nangskagnok. Ma-a paarang
dedrangko batilchi, air-gunchi do-o goramrame roaniko dakjachina ku'pattina nanggen. Ma'gitcham
pagitchamrangde cha'nasa do-o mat goa; an'sengnade ong ja.
Pa Llewellyn-ni kitap do-orangni bidingo u-ie ra'na an-chingna chol on'a. Ian rama bakchatani,
buring o-sapanisa. Chasong gitalni chadamberangna rama ja-kol ja-rikna ia ki-tap mesoka.
Pa Llewellyn, kitaprangko poraie aro an-tangan buring nape a-bri chiring doe sandie niesa ia
ki-tapko sea ine nikna man-a. Noksarangko sale mesokani gimin do-orangni jat - ma-malko u-ie ra pana ua
an-chingna chol ong-a. A.chik Asongni bol wa-arang, matburingrang aro na'tokrangni biding aro gipin katta
bichongrangko ra'e sea - jotanirangko dakdapangkuna una Nokgipa chol aro be'enni an'senganiko
on'angkuchina.
Ia ki-tapna Aganpaaniko dakna angna on-ahanina, anga Pangrakpako mittelbea.
