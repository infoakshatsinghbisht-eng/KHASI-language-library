A'chikrangni bidingo rokom rokom A'chikkuchi seanirang ka'sne
ka'sne bang'rorobaengha. Indioba chugipa gadangrango poraigiparangna
aro skia man'gipa manderangna Seanirang komibeenga. Ia
ka'dongsona man'a.
Jatprakni gimin rokom rokom seani dongna nanggen. Uamangni
daka-rikani, cha'bewal-robewalni aro agangipa ku'sikrangni gimin seani
gipin manderang ma'sina neng'gen, aro iandakgipa rokom rokom
mangmang chanchie sesretanirang dongangaha. Uni gimin jattangni
chong'motgipa noksako on'na skia man'giparangni kam ong'a.
Ia kitapko anga "A'chikkuni Ma'ambi" ine done, A'chikkuni
a'bachengbaani, uni ja'pang-ja'dil aro gipinrangni Kattarangko dingtang
dingtang chasongrango jakkalchapbaani giminsan mangmang seaha.
A'chikkuni gam'ani bewalrang (Phonetics), Sentencerangni dingtang
ong'ani (morphology), A'chikku Grammar aro Sentencerangko rikani
bewalrang (Syntax) ko iano on'tisaba janapa dongja maina uarangko
sena gita uandakgipa subjectko poraigipa mande ong'na nanggen indiba
indakgiparangko seanirang dongjaskal A'chik ku'sikko rongtale u'ina
man'jawa.
