AGANCHENGANI
A.chikrang badita chasong A-song Chigako
dongna nangachim aro A.chikrangni jat chigni
changiparangoni badiagipa jat da.o songdongenggipa
A.chik songo songdongechengachim ba napechengachim
uni gimin darangba kakket dake aganna ba mesokna
manj.a. Indiba Achikrangni songdongachabaaniko
ma-gitcham pagitchamrangni ku-riting aro daororo
Achikrangni songdong achabaaniko niede, A.chikrang
ong-achim ine nikna man-a. A.chikrangni indake
janggitangbaanio chanchie aro so.sojengjeng dake
janggitangbaani bano maidake bon.chota uarangko
am-aonian ia novel da.o nikenggipa bimangona sokaha.
Novelo parakgipa pilak ‘character'- rangan chanchichipe
banaigipasan ong-a. Badita chasong Zaminderrang
A.chikrangko rokomari dake sinjetbaengachim aro ua
sinjetanirang bano maidake bon-aha iarangko ia novelo
chanchichipe seanisam ong-aia. A.chikrang gipinni nokkol
itani aro chonik- onchepe jakkalani ka-ako (bitterness)
ka.tongo dingchike rae Zaminderrang baksa dakgrikna
ka.kim chabamitingo ‘East India comapny' -ba on-tisa
rengsi man.paeaha. Britishrang sokbakujaode A-chikrangni
katong wa.al chaani, Zaminderrangni kosako ka.kim
chae jingjengatani bilongbegenchim. Indiba Britishrang

AGANSAMSOANI
Jeo nangni gisik, unon nangni gam ong-a. Na.a
mainaba gisik nangchongmotode aro uko gisik nange
dakode, uan nang-ni aro jatni gam ong-a. Na-a ki.tap
sena gisik nangani dongode aro gisik nangen seode, uan
nangni aro jatni gam ong-a.
