TALATCHENGANI
Iana skang anga Ku-sikni bidingo an·tangni seanirangko
bichong on-e ki-tap nakatataha. Iano Ku-sikni bidingo ong.gija,
Literature ba Sea-Jotani bidingo an-tangni seanirangko mingchikking
ra-dime,"Sea-Jotani Bidingo Seanirang" ine katta bichong on-e
chappa ka-attaienga.
Iano man-chapatgipa seanirang mingprakni gimin kan-dik
kan-dik aganna ska: (n) "Sea-Jotanio Uie Ra-na Nanggipa Kattarang"
essayo, Englisho sea-jotanio jakkalronggipa, indiba A.chikkuode
a-gittalgipa kattarangni bidingo talataniko daka. (2) Sea bewal-
rokomko nitoatna aro knatoatan baksana nikjagringatna, aganani-
scani bimangrangko senokgiparang jakkalronga. Gnigipa essay
"Aganani Bimangrang"-o indakgipa Aganani Bimangrangko talata.
(3) Poedo seaniosan ong-aigija gipin rokomni seanirangoba tosusae
agananirangko jakkalbea, maina iaranga seaniko wa-saatan baksana,
nikjagringatachitalbatatna dakchaka."A.chikku Poedorango Tosusae
Agananirang"-o A.chikku poedorango jakkalgipa tosusae
agananirangni bidingosan seaia. Ia tosusae agananirangna rokomari
rakgipa kattarang donga. Indiba ua kattarangko iano jakkalja, maina
ia seaniko ramram gadango uie ra-pana sikgiparangnasa miksonga.
(4) An.ching A.chikrang me-chik poedo segipakode Jonmoni D.
Shirakosan uitokpaaia. Indiba gipin gipin poedo sena changgipa
A.chik me-chikrang gnang. Uamangni poedorang Jonmoni D. Shirani
seanirangkoba nikchangskanade manja. Ia scanio uamangni
on-gilaniko ra.chaka aro niksengata. Iamangni ja-mano poedo
segiparangko iano man.chapja. (5)"A.chikku Romantic Poetry", ian
ruuta bilsirangna skang segipasa. Ia seanio'romantic poetry'-ni gimin
on-tisa talatchenge, ia dolo ga-akgipa A-chikku poedorangko nia. (6)
"Kan-dikgipa Golporang" essayo, mai rokom seaniko short story ba
Kan-dikgipa Golpo ine sea-jotanio minga, uko talatna jotton ka-a.
Golpoko kan-dike seaiode, uno Kan.dikgipa Golponi gunrang
dongjaode, uko Kan.dikgipa Golpo ine aganna man.ja. Kan.dikgipa
Golponi gunrangko ia seanio talata. (7) Snigipa essayara “Kabe". Kabe-

gimin ning-tue apale ong.jaoba, A.chikkuo sea-jotani bidingo
kosakgilgilde an-ching pilakan uie ra-na nangchongmota.

AGANPAANI KATTARANG
A.chik ku-siko sea-jotanirang, gipin jatrang baksa tosusaode
namen komibe-engkua ine da-alona kingkingba aganna man-a. Una
bateba A-chikni sea-jotani bidingo sandie, chanchibewale seanirangde
ki-taprangko A.chik chanchibewalrakgipa manderang nakatatgnok,
gisiktango nisengsoe dongengachim. Segiminrangni gimin ning·tue
chanchie, seani bewalrangni gimin u.i-ma.sie, segiparangni
chanchiani bidingrangni gimin chanchibewalskae uamangni
miksongani, gisikna mesokania maikai rokom rokom
chanchianirangko, ma·sianirangko manderangni ka-tongo nabaata
Unon pringsao Ma. B. K. Sangma an-tangni segimin ki-tapna "Sea-
jotani Pidingo Seanirang"-na talatchenganiko see on-pachina tele-
phone ka-e ango molmolaha aro uni segiminko ki-tap dakna
tarigiminko angona watataha.
Uni segimin ki-tapko poraie nion angni sintie, nichaksoe
dongenganina Nokgipa angna aganchakaha gita anga
chanchimanpila. Gipin segimin A.chikni sea-jotani bidingo
segiminrangna bate Ma. B. K. Sangma namen A.chik ku-siko segimin
aro chapa ka-gimin ki-taprangko sandirikkote seaniko anga
chagrongaha. Ma. B. K. Sangma an-tangan English Literatureko skie
ragipa aro Tura Government Collegeo bang-a bilsirangna skie on:gipa
taridape nambegipa ki-tapko dakna cholko ra-aha.

namnamgipa poedorang (Ku-minchi), dakmesokanirang, Donmitape
seanirang aro ring-ringanirang, ma.ringanirangni gimin Ma. B. K
Sangmani sandianiko aro ma-sisokaniko aro una agreba gital gital
nikanirangko aro chanchianirangko niksoe anga an-tangba bang.a
ma-sidapanirangko man-aha.
Ma. B. K. Sangma indakgipa ki-tapko sena ru-uta bilsirangko ra.e
chanchibewalna, chanchirikkotna aro uarangko sea-jotani bimangona
