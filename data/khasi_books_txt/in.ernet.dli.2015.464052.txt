AGANCHENGANI
"Scrooge aro Christmasni Gisikrang" -ian pe-skagimin golposa.
Charles Dickensni segipa "A Christmas Carol" -ko Rani Sircar
bi-sarangna poraitona gita altukale aro bakkandikkale aro katta
bichongko on-tisa dingtangate, seskaaha. "Scrooge aro Christmasni
Gisikrang" -ko ia seskagiminonikosa pe-skaa.
Charles Dickens 1812 oni 1870 ona janggi tanggipa, songsarni
golporangko sena changbegipa British mande ong-achim. Uni pagipa
gro nangstipe pattoko dongani gimin, ua bilsi 12 onin karkanao kam
ka-mitingo uni nie-nike uie-ma-sie ra-anirang uni golpo seanirangna
namgipapangchakanikoon-aha.
Uni
seanirangoni
mongsongbatgiparanga A Christmas Carol, David Copperfield, Oliver
Twist, A Tale of Two Cities, Pickwick Papers aro Great Expectations.
A Christmas Carolara alaksibegipa akotibegipa mande Ebenezer
Scroogeni aro Christmasni salsa skanggipa attamo uni me-mangrangko
gisikrangko chagrongani golpo, jean bilsini bilsirangna dal-a chona
a-gilsakni pilakkon gisik nangatan baksana kusi ong-atenga. Dickens
ia golpoko 1843 bilsio seahaoba, uko 1915 bilsiosa chappa
ka-atchengaha. Manderang namnikbeani gimin, ia golpoko changni
chang chappa ka-taitaikuaienga.
Scrooge kattani ortoara alaksibegipa, akotibegipa. Ebenezer
alaksibeani gimin uko Scrooge minga ongja. Ebenezer Scrooge
alaksibegipa mande ong-ani giminsa, alaksibegipa manderangna uni
bimingko jakkala. Indake English katta "Scrooge" Charles Dickensni
golpoo mongsonggipa bakko ragipa Scrooge-onisa ong.kata.

Ku-gipinni kattarangna basakobade ku-tango orto apsangipa
kattarangko man-ja. Indake ong-on, apsananggipa kattarangko ba adita
kattarangko dondime talatgipakosa jakkalna ga-aka. Tangkana katta
jakkalaba dingtangronga aro mitam obostarang aro bosturangde
pe-skaenggipani samtangtango aro ku-siko dongjanaba donga. Unon
pe-skaenggipa ku-tango dongipa gipin apsananggipa kattarangko aro
uni samtangtango donggipa bosturangni bimingrangko on·skaa.
Pe-skagimin seani, dongbringipa songsaro, pe-skaenggipani
ku-siko sea-jotanirangni gisepo ra.chakako man-na nanggen. Un
baksanaba, ua seani 'individuality' ba uni dingtangmancha ong aniko
gimaatna nangjawa. Sechenggipa golponi gun aro su-uanikoba
peskaanio jaktuatjana jott on ka-anikoba nangchongmota. Ia pilakko
nigopesa, pe-skagipa kattarangni ortoko pe-a aro ku-gipino donskaa.
Indake pe-skaaniara, segital-rugitalani gunkoba parakani ong-a.
A-gilsak da-o chonrikrikangenga. Re-a-doani aro ku-agangrikani,
kobor watgrikani cholrang nambatbeaha aro tang.dobeahani gimin,
meli-nangrime, sambabatgrike janggi tangania, ku-sik man'grikgijanichi
peng:panganiko ra-galna nanganiko ong-katata. Namgipa chugipa
gadango seanirangko ku'gipino pe-skaaniba, ia peng-panganiko
ragalani chol mingsa onga.
Namgipa seaniko poraigakmanode, uko gipinrangna rengsi
tikatna skani bang-a manderangon gnang. Indaka gisikan angkoba ia
golpoko pe-skae dal-giminrangona aro chongiparangonaba sokatna
skataha. Poraigiparangko suk ong-atjana ba gisik dongtoatjanade iako
seja, indiba uamangni gisiko maiba chanchianiko nakatatnasa sea,
ine Dickens uni Aganchenganio janapa. Golpo nambeani gisik
gisikko, Dickensni miksonga gitan, su-dikchongmota. An·tangtangni
gisikni re-aniko aro janggi tanganiko nipilatnaba ia golpo uko
poraigiparangna do-ga oe on-a.

Uan Christmasna salsagipani attamchim aro ua attamara
sin-begipa attam ongachim. Sinbea aro guurirara,
ka-sinbegipa ka-namgijagipa balwani balara manderangni
mikkangrango su-a gita nangchakachim aro grengrangko
tin-kaata gita dakpilachim. Salgimikan sal ontisamangba
najachim aro da-o, songjinmani ghori dal-giparang gittam
bajikosan dokataiengkuoba, andalgopaiahachim. Officerango
aro dokanrango bijoli chingataha aro sorokni
teng-atanirangkoba teng-ataha. Indiba guuri mitmatdugaani
gimin teng-ata teng-atja gita dakaiaha; batesa samtangtangni
andalaniko andaldapataha. Pilakchin guuri, a-paloba aro
nokningoba.
Indiba guurioba, singekgekoba aro balwa ka-namjaoba,
Christmas Eve-de Christmas Eve. Manderang kamtangtangko
dakmitingo gisik ritchengachim aro kusi ong-achim, aro
dokanrangba bang-en palnangaha. Kusi ong-ani salko manina
gita boksisrangko brena nangachim aro cha-ani, ringanirang
aro rokomari bosturang manderangna brena nanggiparang
bang-achim. Wil-wat pil-pat mande gapao, saksa sakgipinko
grongdike me-a me-chikrang “Merry Christmas, Mery
Christmas" ine salam ka-griktokaha. Banoba banoba, sorok
teng-atgipani ja-pangrango ba dokan cholgugarango
chadengsroke, salgipino cha-nasienggipa Christmasni
meatamni gimin chanchijoljole, bi-sarang Christmasni
gitrangko ring-engachim.

Uni gimin, ia ka-namgijagipa, alaksibegipa mandeo
ripengska donggijaniara aiao inmanani ongja. Uni

Indakarangkoba uade nikskajachim; ua an-tangni
jaksramgijani kamrango jakchakgija ong-e dongaiachim.
Indake, gisiktango Christmasni an-saoanina on-tisaba

ka-namgija ka-tong ua sin-begipa dringon haida katchaakon.
