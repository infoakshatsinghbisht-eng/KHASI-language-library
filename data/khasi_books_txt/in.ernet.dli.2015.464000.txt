Beng'blokrang Jripengaha
Da'a bilside chibolrangoba beng'blok mikoakon bang'e
knajajok. Bengbong mikoakode knaroroangsrangja'engjok.
Mikka wakarioba beng'blokkon nikna man'angjaengjok
Bengbong aro beng'blok kusimtoksan ong'ja, beng'blok,
wapek, bengbong chongiparangkoba nikangjaengjok. Uarang
gimaroroangengaha. Songo mande jelbae beng'blok
roronggipa chibolrangan bon'angengaha aro chibilokrangba
komiangengahai gimin ong'akon. Ia beng'blokni golporangko
songni pante sakbri, Tongkam, Mingsing, Pajim aro Kangrak
indake betbetangengachim. "Skango angni chonmitingode
ching nok ka'ma ramao chibol dakgipaon, mikka wabajokode
walgimik gimik ka'supile bengbong aro beng'blokrang
mikorongachim. Da'ode chibolba dapa gita dakjok aro
tong'sakode skul nok rikjok. Maidake beng'blokrang
tanggen?" ine Tongkam ripengtangrangna aganengachim.

Wal'tim Chaka
Sin'kari sokbaode manderang wal'tim chakna suk
ong'nikbea. A'chik songrangode me'apa me'chikma, pante
me'tra, bi'sa disa wal'tim chakna namnika. Taping pagipaba
December a'bachengaon nokjanggilo sa'ra patalo wal'tim
chakna nokkingko tin pine tariaha aro am'bolrangkoba bol
ja'dil aro gantong dal'dalarangko chimonge dane
donengachim. Taping pagipako dakchakaha. Joltango
donggiparang Tapingma'ang nokona wal'timchakna
re'barongachim. Waltingaonan wal'tim chake golpo ka'e
rorongachim. Taping ma'gipa wal'tim chakgipa sambao
chu'dap tarie ta'milangrang aro ta'bolchu aro uandake ta'a
bipekrangko rite wal'tim chakgiparangna on'rongachim. Cha
gitchak dakeba on'rongachim. Taping dokta nambregijarangko
asongchakna am'pok ro'roe tarie donaha. Manderang wal'tim
chake bal'eke ka'dinge rooba mande skako mitake aro gisik
saani kattarangko aganede roskajachim. Tapingni ma'a paa
jaksrama aro mande ska baksa melie dongrongachim. Uni
