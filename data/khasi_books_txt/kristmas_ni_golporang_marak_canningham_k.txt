1. The Child of Love:- 
This is the story of a mother telling her six year

old daughter Kasha about the meaning of Christ- 
mas and God’s gift of His Child of love Jesus Christ 
to mankind as Saviour and how all the earthly gifts 
received are difterent from God’s kind of Gift and 
the mother giving her dau^ter’s heart as gift to 
Jesus. 
2. The Frogs are Silent: - 
The story narrates how due to the destruction of 
habitat and killing, the frogs are decreasing in num- 
ber and their croaking are hardly heard, and how 
to save the frogs from destruction, four friends from 
the village took the initiative along with the vil- 
lage folks to create a new habitat for the diminish- 
ing frogs and how this attempt to save the ftogs 
from extinction became successful. 
3. A Pot of Rice Flour Sesame steamed Cake:- 
This story is the story of one girl Patchi who along 
with her kind and generous mother took the 
steamed rice cakes and pithas from glutinous sticky 
rice to the poor and needy in the true essence of 
the Christmas spirit. 
4. Warming over the Winter Fire:- 
This is the story of a family who used to welcome

blessings of God for the generousity never cease. 
5. The Charm and Magic of Christmas:- 
When the church, the vicinity and the homes were 
decorated with the lights, twinkling bubbles, glitzy 
tinsels, nobody could resist the beauty and the

lovely shine these decorative lights emanated. The 
charm and magic of these bright beckoning lights 
touched the hearts of two drunkard strangers who 
found their ways inside the church and found peace 
and change of heart which only the light of Jesus 
could transform. This is how the story runs. 
6. A Bowl of Rice for Grandma:- 
The story narrates the warmth, generousity, care 
and concern of the church members of the village 
community headed by their pastor who remem- 
bered to share the portion from the community feast 
to those who were sick, their families, the needy 
and the downtrodden with the true message of 
Christian love and brotherhood.
