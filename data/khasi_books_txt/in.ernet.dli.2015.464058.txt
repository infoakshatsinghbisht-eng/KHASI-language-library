AGANCHENGANI
"Sisobrao Jumang" ine chinga ia ki-tap bi-sani bimingko
donna namnikaha. Sisobrao Jumangara ia kitapo
man-chapatgipa seanirangoni mingsa ong.oba, ianon Ma
Fridina Koknal Marakni gisikni re-ani, jatna a-songna ka-saani,
A.chikni dakbewal-rikbewalko ripingna skani, seani
ritingantion, sentence ge-antion jojranga. Uni gimin chinga
iakon katta bichongna kranikbea.
Adita bilsirangna skang, Shillongo kam ka-gipa A.chik
me.chik Officerrang Dol ge-sako bikote, una Noringro Nojingjo
Organisation (N.N.O.) ine biming on-aha. Noringro Nojingjo-
ni ortoara "Aski Tom-daksa". Ia Dolni skanggipa Presidentara
Ma Fridina Koknal Marak. Gipin member-rangara Ma Hero-
ine Bangshall Sangma, Ma Debilla Bolwary Marak, Ma Anita
Lizana K. Sangma, Ma Verna K. Sangma, Ma Kamala Raksam
Marak, Ma Bharati B. Sangma, Ma Withmollish Mangsang
Sangma, Ma Isabella D. Marak, Ma Hannah Lee Kin Kaid
Cheran Momin, Ma Hemalata Chambugong Marak, Ma
Mirnaloy Dajel Marak, Ma Jahanara Koksi Sangma, Ma Keziah
Me.cheng Sangma, Dr. Kumie Raksam Marak aro Ma Brucellish
Koksi Sangma.
Dolni manderang an-tangtang bang-a bang.ja on-grime,
A-chikska-rangoniko gile aro Gauhatini Assam Academy For
Cultural Relations-ni, Pa Harendra Wa-ge Marakni
tom-chimonggimin Ku-bidikko chapa ka-atgipako bree lapna
palskae adita tangkarangko chimongaha. Uchin Dolni nang-
chongmotgipa bosturangko brechenge, A.chikkuo segimin
ki-taprangko aro A.chikrangni rokomari jako gitoko
gananirangko aro rangrangko bree chimongaha. Adita
susaanirangko A.chik bi.sarangnaba dakate boksisrangko
on-aha.
Indiba brikalgipa memberrang kamoni nengtake
re-bildangtokahaon, ia Dol be-gruange, ja-mano dongsrangjaha.

Adita bilsirangni ja-mano Turao donggipa member
gitchamrang, N.N.O.-ni bosturangko pale aro Banko donggipa
tangkako oksrange, bribatgipa member sakgittamni sea-
nirangko ki-tap chapa ka-atskana tom-e chanchiaha. Ia somoeo
Ma Kamala Marakba DIET-ni Principal ong-e Turaona
re-baaha; indake bini dakchakaniko bang-en man-aha.
Ma Fridina aro Ma Heroine re-rorona bil dongjahani
gimin, Dr. Kumie aro Ma Brucellish baditaba chang re-anga
re-baa dake, ku.pattianiko ra-rurae, agangrikanirangko
matchotataha. Indake Ma Fridina, Ma Heroine aro Ma Vernani
seanirangko ki-tap dakna tik ka-manchaaha. Shillongchi
re-angarango gipin memberrangko gronge, gronggi-
jarangminga phonechi agangrike aro grongsranggi-
jagiparangba ia tik ka-aniko ra-chakpaaigen ine ka-donge, ki-tap
dakna chanchianiko kamo chu-sokatna ja-ku gaaiaha. Ian
2oo9-ni a-bachengachibara.
Dr. Kumie aro Ma Brucellish, Ma Fridinako grongna
re-angon, ua an-tangan basee uni seanirangko chingna
on-ataha. Badiabarangde sechenggipako tong.kandi
setaienggiparangsa, mitama on-jotani aro namatpilani
donggiparangsa ong-ani gimin, seanirangko tik bimango
dontaina nangahae, ki-tapko nakatatningna man.jaha. Chingni
uko grongtaina re-angon, Ma Fridina skanggipa bilonge saaoni
nambapilenga. Ua an-sengsrangahaon, gipin seanirangko basee
mingsa minggni on.dapkugen ine aganachim. Neng-taksrange
ja-mano basena man-aigen ine chingni aganon, ua bakan
an-sengpilaigen aro basee on.dapatna man-aigen ine
aganchaka. Indiba ia chol chingona sokbana skangan uko
Nokgipa Isol neng-takkamsrangkan rimangaha. Ma Fridina
ia ki-tapko nikna baditan kusi ong-begenchim.
Garo Graduates' Unionni 2oo9-ni “Mikaoa" magazineo,
Ma Brucellish Ma Fridinani photoko on-chape uni gimin sea.
