Koasi Mindini - Songgitcham:
Koasi Mindini, Te.brong Sangsim, Ola Olding
pagitchamrangni songdong a:chagipa songgitchamrangoni
gitchambatsranggipa song damsa onga. Pagitchamrangoni
bang batan, Mande unia Mitdeni ma-ambiko katta chengo bima
kolo, Koasi Mindinio Te-brong Sangsim Ola Oldingo Mande
ming Mitde apsan songdong a'charimaonisa chengteke
aganronga. Ua songgitchamoan Katchi Mitde Melaram unia
Mitde Mande Susaramba donga. Sume aro Dome abisaba ua
songgitchamo songdong achachenggipa A-chik pagitcham
ma'gitcham nokdangrangoni nokchongsa onga. Sumeni
segipako Ruba, unia Dambeni segipako Jonja minga. Sume
aro Dambeni demechikranga Dome Ramse abisa, unia
depanteranga Dikrang Diksang adasa onga.
Dikrang Diksang adasa Na'garam Susaram Truma
Bawilramo naek na'tokko jikchenggiparang ong'a. Bisong
adasasa chiakol cho.chenga, china digi kitchenga, gogaiko
dokchenga, unia Il-dek chiko ringchenga chong'motan, Il-dek
chiko ringe gogaichenggiparang ong'a. Ua Na'garam Susaram
Truma Bawilramkon Katchi Mitde Melaram Mitde Mande
Susaram ineba minga.
Dome aro Ramse abisani sedranga Suja ming Donja,
mamatangranga Koepa ming Bamilpa onga. Uamangni
Naigaram Susaram Truma Bawilramo songdonge, Ildek
chibimaoniko na-ek jike, unia uko chi ringe gogai doke
saksako bajokna." Unosa Marak Makal adasa ming Sangma

Rongro Rongkimieng, Chiancheng Dachireng:
Rongro Rongkimjeng Chiancheng Dachireng A-chik
pagitchamrangni songgitcham damsa ong'a. Ma-ambi aganna
mangiparang, Doro ra'gipa unia Chera solgipa me-aparang
Rongro Rongkimjeng Chiancheng Dachirengko
mingchapronga. Mongsongbate, chengoni pagitchamrangoni
gangipa potgipa unia man'gipa pakgipani gimin talaton Rongro
Rongkimjeng song unia Chiancheng Dachireng chibimani
gimin janapchapronga. Rongmituni ma-bitchi a:gipirangoni,
Sokre ming Dimre abisa unia adatangrang Roma ming Rojing
adasa, uamangba Rongro Rongkimjengo songdong
a'chachenggipa unia Chiancheng Dachirengo ja'andileng
(ja-anchinik) witchenggipa unia kni gisi galchenggiparang
onga.
