KI SYIEM KHASI BAD SYNTENG
Da Dr. H. Lyngdoh (1937)
Ka Bynta 1: Ka Thymmei Ki Syiem:
Ka synshar Hima ha ka Ri Khasi kam shym la mih na ka bor jong ka waitlam, hynrei na ka jingiatreilang jong ki Kur bakhraw kiba la thaw ia ka Hima. Ki Syiem Khasi ki dei ki 'Syiem Blei' kiba pyniaid ia ka Hima da ka Hok ryngkat ki Myntri, ki Lyngdoh, ki Basan, bad ki Sirdar.
Ka Bynta 2: Ki 25 Tylli ki Hima Khasi:
Ka Ri Khasi ka don 25 tylli ki Hima kiba laitluid, kynthup ia ka Sohra, Khyrim, Mylliem, Nongkhlaw, Mawiang, Nobosohphoh, Maharam, Langrin, Mawkyllei, bad kiwei pat. Ha man la ka Hima, u Syiem um lah ban rai marwei; u dei ban pynlong ia ka Dorbar Hima.
Ka Bynta 3: Ka Hima Jaintia (Sutnga):
Ha Ri Jaintia (Pnar), u Syiem Sutnga u la pyniaid ia ka Hima kaba heh naduh ki lum haduh ki them Sylhet. Ki 12 ngut ki Daloi ki la long ki nongsynshar ia ki Elaka bapher bapher kiba la bat ia ka dustur bad ka niam tynrai jong u Pnar.
