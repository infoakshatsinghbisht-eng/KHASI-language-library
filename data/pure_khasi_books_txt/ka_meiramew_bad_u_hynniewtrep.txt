As far as I can recollect from the stories
of my childhood, in the earlier days peo-
ple did not dare to violate the ancient
customs because they firmly believed that
they were good for the land and the

inheritance of properties as the people, in
my opinion, out of the evil of their hearts,
greedy for benefits to satisfy their selfish
interests, have no more respect for the
ancient customs and no more care or
thought for others.
