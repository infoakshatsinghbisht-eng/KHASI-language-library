KAEI KA KOLSHOR?
Ka kyntien 'Kolshor' ka long ka kyntien kaba lar shibun
bad kaba eh ban ai tang ha ka shi kyntien la ka jmgmut
kaba don ha ka Watia ki don shibun napdeng ki nongthoh Khasi
ktba la pyrshang ban ai la ka jong ka jingbatai la ka jingmut jongka

Don napdeng jong ki, kiba la khot da ka 'jymbriew' bad ka 'dustur'
lane 'ritiV hynrei kine baroh ki dei tang ki jingtyrwa, namar kiba
bun hi ki dangiapdiang ban pyndonkam da ka kyntien 'kolshor'
Ka kyntien 'kolshor' kam dei ka ktien khasi tynrai, hynrei la
shu shim kylliang la ka na kiwei pat ki jait kyntien ban pyndap
pynbiang la ki jingdonkam ha ka kren ka khana bad ka thoh ka
pule Kane ka kyntien ka wan na ka ktien Phareng 'Culture' bad
kaba ki Phareng pat ki shim kylliang na ka ktien Latin 'Cultura'
bad ka jingmut tynrai jong kane ka kyntien ka long ban thung ban
tep ban loh la u symbai ba la btep ne u tynrai ba la thung " Kane ka
kyntien ka mut ruh "ka jingpynlong pynman" Kumta ngi
sngewthuh ba kane ka kyntien ka mut la kaei kaei kaba la da
pynsan la ka.
Ha ka rukom pyndonkam ba mynta, ka kyntien 'kolshor' ka
wan na ka ktien German 'Kultur' kaba thew la ka rukom im jong ki
bnew Del na kane ka kyntien ba ki Phareng ki ju ong 'Cultured
person' ka ba mut la ka jmglong ba la nang la shai u bnew uba la
shah hikai ha ka hang ka akor buro m
Ban kham shai shuh shuh halor ka jingmut ka kyntien kolshor

ngin la phai sha ki Jingbatai jong ki artthropologist. U E. B. Tylor u
batai kumne kumba la pynwan sha ka ktien Khasi da Ka Philomena
Kharakor :
"Ka kolshor ka dei kata ka tlieng ba la thain lang da ki
symboh jingtip Ki symboh jingngeit, ki symboh jingstad
bad ki symboh tipbriew tip-Blei, ka aifi ka dustur bad
kiwei kiwei pat ki jait jingmhen ba u bnew u kynshew
kum u dkhot jong ka kynhun imlang sahlang".
Culture IS that complex whole which include
knowledge, beliefs, arts, morals, law, customs and other
capabilities and habits acquired by man as a member of
society
Katei ka jingbatai jong u Tylor ka long halor ka kolshor kaba
la san lypa, kaba u bnew u loh kum u dkhot ka kynhun imlang
sahlang U bnew u loh la ktne kiba u Tylor u thoh kum ka jingtip,
ka jingngeit, ka buitthaw, ka akor burom, ka am bad kiwei kiwei
pat ki Jingmhen, na ka imlang sahlang Haba u ong 'kiwei kiwei
pat' u mut lehse la kiwei pat ki bynta ka kolshor ki bym lah ban jer
lut Dei halor kane ka jingbatai ba kiwei kiwei pat ki nongthoh ki
pynshong nongrim la ki jingbatai jong ki
Ha katei ka jingbatai u Tylor, ka paw ba ka kolshor kam dei ka
snap tipthuh (instinct) kaba don ha u bnew naduh dangkha,'

hynrei kata la kaba u lum, u kynshew bad u loh na kata ka imlang
sahlang ne jaitbynriew la jong
Ka Carol Ember bad u Melvin Ember ki batai kumne
"Ka kolshor ka long ka thup jong kiei kiei ba la nang, kiba
kynthup la ka jingmiien ka jingngeit, ki jinglong, ki dor
lane jingmut ba janai kiba shem ha kano kano ka
imlang sahlang ne ka kynhun bnew".
Culture IS the set of learned behaviours beliefs,
attitudes, values, or ideas that are characteristic of a
particular society or population
Kat kum katei ka jingbatai, shisha u bnew ba un nang la kano
kano ka kam u dei ban da pynmiien bad ban da shah hikai shuwa
Ynda u la shah hikai, u la nang. Nangta u mlien ban leh bad ban lai
leh ruh la kaei kaba u la nang. Sa kawei ka jingbatai jong kitei ki
nongthoh ka long ba ka kolshor ka dei ka bynta jong ka imlang
sahlang jong u ba bun ba lang ne jong ka kynhun briew Tang uwei
u briew ne kawei ka briew kim lah ban pynlong kolshor Kata ka
jingmlien ka kylla long kolshor ynda haba la leh da kata ka kynhun
briew
U Ralph Linton u batai kumne
"Ka kolshor ka kdew la ka dur jmgim ba pura jong ka

kynhun briew ba la long lang, ymdei shuh tang la ki bynta
jong kane ka dur jingim, la kaba ka imlang sahlang ka hiew
kham halor lane kham sngew donkam "
Culture refers to the total way of life of any society, not
simply to those parts of this way which the society
regards as higher or more desirable
Katei ka jingbatai u Ralph Linton ka kdew hi ba ka kolshor ka
del ka rukom im jong kano kano ka kynhun briew bad kata ka
rukom im ka mih na ka jmgpynmlien ban im bad ban leh kumta
Imat del na kane ka jingbatai jong u Ralph Linton ba u J S
Shangpliang u loh jingmut bad pynlyngkot la la ka jingbatai da
kaba ong ba ka kolshor "Ka thew la ka longrynieng jong u briew ne
ka jaitbynriew"
Uwei pat u riewstad jong ka anthropology, u William A
Haviland u batai kumne
"Ka kolshor ka dei ka thup jong ki aih lane ka kyrdan
kaba haba la leh da kita kiba long ki dkhot jong ka imlang
sahlang ka pynmih la kata ka j'ingmlien kaba ladei lang
ban leh wat kham pher ruh tang hapoh u pud jong ka, la
kaba ki dkhot jong kata ka kynhun ki hiew kum kaba dei
biang bad kaba lah ban la pdiang lang"

Culture IS the set of rules or standard that when acted
upon by the members of a society, produce behaviour
that falls within a range of variance the members
consider proper and acceptable
Kat kum katei ka jingbatai jong u Haviland, ka kolshor ka dei
hi ka jingmlien ne ka rukom leh. bad kata ka rukom leh, u shim Ta
kata kaba long pdeng bad kaba dang dei hi hapoh u pud jong ka.
Kata ka jingleh ka kylla long kolshor ynda haba la leh lang ne
pdiang lang da baroh kita kiba long ki dkhot jong kata ka kynhun
Hangne ngi lohi ba u Haviland u shim kumjuh kumba la batai ka
Carol Ember bad u Melvin Ember bad bun kiwei pat, ba ka kolshor
ka del ka jong uba bun ba lang Tang uwei ne kawei ka briew kim
lah ban pynlong kolshor, hynrei dei ynda haba la kano kano kaba
uwei u leh, ryngkat bad uba bun ba lang ne kata ka kynhun briew
ka pdiang bad leh lang, kata ka jingleh ka kylla long ka kolshor ha
kata ka jaitbynriew ne ka kynhun bynriew
Lada ngin shim pat la ka jingbatai u E Andamson Hoebel,
une ruh u shim ba ka kolshor ka dei ka jinglumlang la ki jingmlien
ba kyrpang bad ki kylla kolshor haba la leh ne pdiang da ka imlang

Jingmlien ba kyrpang Ka dei ka jingpynlong kawei la ki
Jingmlien ba la nang, la ki ba la leh bad la loh bynta lang

(" .. Culture IS more than a collection of mere isolated
bits of behaviour It is the integrated sum total of
learned behaviour traits which are manifest and
shared by member of a society"
Hangne u Adamson ruh u kren la ka kolshor kaba la san lypa
Ka don ka jingiasyriem la ka jigbatai u Haviland ba kita ki jingmlien

U Marvin Harris pat u ong:

la hikai bad Toh pateng da ka kynhun briew kaba ngi
shem ha ka imlang sahlang jong u briew"
Culture refers to the learned socially acquired tradition
of thought and behaviour found in human societies
Une u nongthoh pat u kren la ka kolshor ba ka dei ka jingmlien

— in other words their ideas, behaviours, language
and artifact These includes institutions (such as
