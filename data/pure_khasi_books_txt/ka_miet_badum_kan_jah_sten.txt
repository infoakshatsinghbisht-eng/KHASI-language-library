PREFHE
It is after a research of three years that the present
work could be presented to the readers. Its aim is to
acquaint our young people of some feelings which they
need to know in their dealings with their contemporaries.
Older people, on the other hand, will find in it an inte-
resting review of their own past experiences-golden or
silver, sweet or bitter though they might have been
which they may have forgotten. Therefore, with the law
of contrast as well as of similarity they will now recall
those past experiences.
A well-informed reader may find in it a combination
of the author s own philosophy with some branches of
knowledge, nevertheless my intention has been to make
it a small part of the study of the problems of our young
people.
The works of some western scholars including some
of the classical works of Dr. Orison Sweti Marden have
been of great help to me. All suggestions from the readers

which will make this book even more useful arc cordially
welcomed.

This small book which has been out of circulatiorf'
since 1967, one year after its publication, has been reprin-
ted. This was possible through the interest taken by my
friend, Mr. D. Mawlong who took the care to sec the
proof, and to see the book out. We were aware of the
interest taken by our young friends in the Khasi and
Jaintia Hills when they started enquiring about a few
copies which I did not have.
I did not intend to make Ka Mied Bad am Kan Jab
a piece of literature. To be true to scholars of the day,
it was my strong and silent reaction to the note I received
from the DPI, Assam when I submitted it for financial
assistance that made me publish it at my own cost. The
scholar who studied the manuscript did not show how
the book could be improved, at least his note was prohi-
bitive. I allow it to come out as it was first published
for record, my own record as well as of my friends and
well-wishers. The next edition will certainly retain the
same spirit but will wear a new look, you may call it

a mature one. Till then, it is intended to be read just to
kill the reader's time.
