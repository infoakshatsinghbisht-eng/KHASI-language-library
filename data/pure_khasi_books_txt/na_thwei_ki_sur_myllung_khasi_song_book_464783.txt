There are 21 songs in this song book originally written and composed by 
Chosterfield Khongwir. The tunes of the songs are folk based. The lyrics, poetic in 
nature, are vividly expressed in various themes ranging from love of natme the 
murmuring streams and cascading waterfalls - the joy and sorrow of himaan life 
and the children’s excitement while playing on the green meadow in the spleadid 
afternoons, as well as a woric song showing them always smart to help their 
mothers the vanous chores at home The maternal attention and the childish joy 
are expressed m the lullabies where the mother’s heart is overflowing with 
tenderness as the child throws its little arms round her neck and hugs her with all 
Its strength, the mother feels good as she hums noble tune and everything around 
her IS beautiful 

The notable song with a message to preserve the environment and another 
one entitled ‘Muri Mun’ which has a clarion call to the youth not to feel shy for 
work and respect for the dignity of labour are included in the songbook. The 
traditional folk melody namely ‘Meikha Meikha’ where children pay homage to 
their paternal grandparents, a unique composition of Mr Khongwir, is another 
addition in this book.
