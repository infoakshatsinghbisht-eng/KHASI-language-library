May 24 , 1979 


The life and teachings of Mahatma Gandhi have become 
a new philosophy in the world today. Many books have 
been published on and about the Father of the Indian Na- 
tion, that is, Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi; and many 
leaders of public opinion have been influenced by those. Dr. 
Martin Luther King once wrote to say that it was from 
Gandhi that he learnt his “operational techniques” in lea- 
ding the Black movement The teachings of Mahatma Gan-

dhi have become a topic in the all-India Service examinations, 
and a subject for some university students. There is indeed 
no end in making of books about this great man, but Uba 
Ki Khot U Mahatma by H. Warjri has its own importance 
in the Khasi society whose members have beard about Gan- 
dhi and might have read only a little about him in their own 
mother tongue. This is the first biography (of this length) 
of Gandhiji in the language, and it is going to benefit the 
readers immensely. The book is going to last for a long 
time. 


I am sure some more biographical works will come out 
in the Khasi language from Mr. Warjri who seems to have 
devoted himself to this section of study.
