LONGSHWA MANSHWA. 



SHIKYNTIEN NA U NONGTHOH. 


Ka E-iadaw ne pyniatur masi kaba shisien shisnem ha ki 
katto katne tylli ki shnong ha Rilum Jaintia, hadien ba la dep ka bet 
ka thung kba lane ha shwa ban lum ban lang bad buh'ha ki Thiar ki 
Tyllu, kam long kaei kaei kaba dangthymmai, hynrei kaba ju pynialeh 
da ki longshwa manshwa, da ki Pamen, ki Beimen jong ka 
jaitbynriew Pnar Jaintia naduh hyndai hynthai naduh myndanglung 
ka pyrthei mynba u Mawsiang, u Mawtha ki dangjem kum ka 
'dewbilat kaba dang shu dep khleh. Lane naduh shwa ka por ban 
mih u Mawser, u Mawpylla mynba ki longshwa ki manshwa ki jong 
ngi ki dangthew khaw da u Khri ne da kaba shu kyndup da ka kti. 

Ka E-iadaw ne pyniadaw-pyniatur masi kaba shisien 
shisnem ka dei kawei na ki jingialehkai kaba pynialeh da ki Masi 
Kyrtong ban pynkmen pynbyrngia ia ki nongshong shnong jong ka 
shnong, ka Raij ne ka Elaka.Ka E-iadaw masi ka long ka saindur ba 
la saindur iohi jngai jong ki longshwa manshwa bad kat kum ka 
sainpyrkhat jong ki ym tang ba ka long ka RongTamasa kaba shisngi 
,ka long ruh ka lad ban tei ia ka imlang ka shlang, ka bha ka miat 
lyngba ka jingiakynduh paralok ia kiba ym shym la ia kynduh la 
kham slem. 

Ka pyniadaw masi ka don arjait, kaba shu pyniadaw rong 
sngewbha bad kawei kaba don bynta bad ka Niam. Ha Takhniang 
bad Nongjngi, Elaka Nongjngi kaba don bynta bad ka niam ha shwa 
ki sngi ka pyniadaw masi ki da knia shwa uwei u Blang. 

Ka E-iadaw ne pyniadaw masi kaba shisien shisnem hadien

ba la dep ka bet ka thung kba, kaba la saindur da ki longshwa 



manshwa ka dangneh dangskhem, dangphuh dangphieng kum 
kawei na ki rong tamasa lehkmen. Nga pyrnnih ia kane ka kot 
shaphang ka E-iadaw masi khnang ba ki longdien ki wandien kin 
nangpynneh pynskhem shuh shuh ha ki por ki ban dangwan.

KA IADAW MASI KA RONG BIRIA LEHKMEN Kl LONGSHWA 

MANSHWA. 


Naduh hyndai hynthai kulong kumah naduh ba danglung 
ka pyrthei ki Nongjaintia ki long ki briew kiba ieid ia ki jingialehkai 
( Sports) naduh ki Rangbah, ki Samla haduh ki khynnah. Ha shwa 
ban mih ka jingialehkai ka juk mynta kum ka jingialehkai Bol, Kriket, 
Hocky, Badminton bad kiwei de ki jingialehkai. Ki jingialehkai kaba 
ki longshwa manshwa jong ka jaitbynriew ki sngewtynnat ban 
ialehkai bad pynkmen ia ki Nongpeitkai ka long kum ka " 
Rahmookhrah, langiat-synraijaringbhang, lapam-um, latan -tylle 
( Tug of War), lasiat-thong ( Archery) laslait ( Wrestling), laphet- 
phari ( Race), lapuh Syiar ( Cock Fighting), Nohsakyryiat, larieh-ku- 
ku( Hide and seek), ladot-moo, lasiat Nup, lapdiah ia kynthir( Jump), 
ladat-dein, E-ladaw Muid bad ka E-ladaw Masi. 

Baroh ki jingialehkai kiba la kdew haneng ki long ki

jingialehkai kiba khring briew bad kiba pynkmen pynsngewbha ia 
ki nongpeitkai. Ha kane ka jingthoh yn tehnoh shwa ia kiba bun ki 
jingialehkai kiba ju ialeh ki longshwa manshwa bad yn shim shwa 
tang ia ka E-ladaw Masi ( Bull Fighting) Ka jingpyniadaw Masi rong 
ka don arjait , ka jingpyniadaw rong kaba khlem don jingiadei ne 
don bynta bad ka Niam bad kawei pat ka jingpyniadaw Masi rong 
ka iaid lang bad ka Niam. 

Ha ka Elaka Raliang ka jingpyniadaw Masi rong kaba shisien 
shisnem ha Madan Pamsna kam don bynta eiei bad ka Niam wat 
lada ka don ka suit ka shor da ka 'kiad Urn da u Klong u Skaw da u 
Doloi, u Pator, ki Wasan bad ki Rangbah shnong hapdeng madan 
pyniadaw ha shwa ban sdang ia ka pyniadaw ne pyniatur ia ki Masi 
Kyrtong. Kat kum ka jingiathuh u Doloi ka Elaka Raliang, Doloi C. 
Lyngdoh ha une u Nongthoh ha kane ka 26/7/2014 la ong ba wat 
lada pyndonkam ia ka " Kyiad Pyrnah" ban suit ban shor shwa ban 



sdang ka jingpyniadaw Masi pynban kam don bynta eiei bad ka 
Niam bad kam shym la don kano kano ka jinglum ia u Synniang u 
Bynhei na ki paidbah lane na ki trai Masi kyrtong kiba pynmih 
Kyrtong ki ban pyniadaw ha kum kane ka sngi. 

Ka jingpyniadaw Masi rong ha ka Elaka Shangpung wat lada 
kam don bynta bad jingiadei eiei bad ka Niam, hynrei ka pher 
khyndiat na ka jingpyniadaw Masi rang jong ka Elaka Raliang. Ha 
Elaka Shangpung ha shwa ki sngi ka jingpyniadaw la don ka jinglum 
synniang da u Khaw lane da ka Pisa na ki trai Masi Kyrtong kiba 
don Masi Kyrtong ban pynmih ha ka sngi E-pyniadaw rong lane na 
kato ka Circle kaba don Masi Kyrtong ka ban pyniadaw. Haba ki la 
ioh ia u Khaw na kito ki trai Masi Kyrtong ne na kato ka Circle bad 
na uta u Khaw ki Shet Kyiad bad ia ka Kyiad kaba mih na kata kaba 
ki shet ki pdem la ainam ba ka dei ka "Kyiad pyrnah" ka la niew ba 
ka dei ka Kyiad khuid Jyiad kyntang bad da kane ka Kyiad ki suit ki

shor ha Madan E-ladaw Masi shwa ban sdang ka jingpyniadaw ne 
pyniatur. 

Ha Elaka Nangbah katba lah ban iohlum jingtip da une u 
Nongthoh na ki 'riew Rangbah bad ki riewshemphang jong ka 
shnong la ong ba ka jingpyniadaw Masi rong kaba shisien shisnem 
kam don bynta eiei bad ka Niam bad ka shu long beit ka 
jingpyniadaw Masi rong kaba pynkmen pynsngewbha ia ki 
nongshong shnong shisien shisnem hadien ba ki la dep ka bet ka 
thung ia u Khaw u Kba ha ki Hali ki pynthor jong ki bad kaba ym 
don kano kano ka jingsiew ne ka bairung ia ki Nongpeitkai kiba 
wan peit ia kane ka jingpyniadaw Masi. 

Ka jingpyniadaw Masi rong ha shnong Mynsngat, Elaka u 
Doloi Nartiang ka long ka jingpyniadaw Masi kaba heh tarn ha 
Rilum Jaintia ha kaba ym tang arhar laihar ne arjur lai jur ki Masi 
Kyrtong kiba iakhun, hynrei da ki bun jur ne bun har ki Masi. Hangne

KA SNGI E-IADAW MASI KA SNGI PYNI RONG. 

Ka sngi E-ladaw Masi ka dei ka sngi lehkmen bad ka sngi 
pyni-rong. Ki briew shynrang bad kynthei naduh ki Rangbah, ki 
Longkmie, ki Samla haduh ki Khynnah najan bad na jngai kirn ju 
pep ban wan peitkai bad ka dei ka sngi pyni-rong ha ka riam ka 
beit. Ki Rangbah, ki Longkmie, ki Samla, ki Khynnah bad ki Tymmen 
ki Kro hala ki riam ki riam ne ki Jainphong ki riam ki beit bha. Kiba 
don kiba eni ki riew Rangbah Jlis hala ka riam bad ka Jainspong 
Phali ne Muka. Ki longkmie, ki samla lud ha ka riam Ryndia, Muka, 
Thohsaru, Dhara bad ki jingdengkpieng ha ryndang bad dengkhadu 
ha ki kti. Ha ki por ba mynshwa, shwa ban mih kine ki jingsleh Pon 
bad sieh Powder ba bun jait, ha Khyllongsniuh ki tyosat syntiew 
"SYNTU LATANGJOO" kaba sma iewbih kum ki Pon bad ki Powder 
ka juk mynta. Ki khynnah hala ki Jainphong ba bun rong hala ki 
rang ki rong. Ha ki por kiba kham mynshwa kiba duk ki bym biang 
ki Jain ki Nep ki da ia pan kylliang tang ban ioh deng iohphong ha 
kum kane ka sngi jinglehkmen.
