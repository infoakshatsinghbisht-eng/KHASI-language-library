PREFACE.

Ft has been the earnest desire of those who are 
intorostjd in tli''. development of the Khasi Literature 
that suitable Khasi books should bo prepared or com- 
piled, cspooially, for Classes V, VI, VII and VIII 
of Khasi Schools. And such readers should be those 
written in a good and chaste Khasi language with <a 
pure Khasi style. 1 h ^y should, at the same time, 
contain, as required by the Education Department,

‘ the achievements of science and the accounts of glo- 
rious and noble deeds*’, so as to inculcate high moral 
idea and to stimulate patriotism and love of science in 
the readers.

Keeping the above facts in view, with a deep sense 
of responsibility as a teacher and headmaster of no 
less than 35 years* experience, and a recognised Kh.isL 
author, I have, now, brought out a now Khasi l)ook 
specially meant for classes V and VI with an attempt 
to meet, as far as possible within the limits of our 
resources, the requirements of the curriculum publish- 
ed by the Department in 1952.

It may be safely said that the book has been written 
in the best Khasi language fitly used to express mo- 
dern thoughts and ideas. And the subjects chosen 
and incorporated in the body of the book are sure to 
captivate the mind of the young readers, and through 
reading them, they will, consciously or unconsciously, 
imbibe the high moral values contained therein.

Ill

The book will thus serve as a moral class book 
besides the study of the standard language.

The book written in a compact form will cover a 
two year course of studies. There are materials and 
suggestions in the book for expansion.

It is to be noted here that the Educ^ation Depart'* 
ment prescribed selections from *‘Ka Sngithiang** for 
Class IV of Khasi schools according to the Gazette 
Notification No.80-G, 2l8t. May 1954. And the book 
had been enthusiastically welcomed by the head- 
masters in so far as a very large number of copies of 
the book had been sold out that year. For this 
reason some of the lessons from “ Ka Sngithiang ** are 
included in the new book, “Ka Sngithiang I, No Ka 
Kot-pule Skiil-pdeng, Klass V & VI.’^

It is confidently hoped that this now book will 
enjoy the kind patronage of the progressive minds in 
the country.
