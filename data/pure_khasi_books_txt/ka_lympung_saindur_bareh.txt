FOREWORD
It gives me pleasure to introduce this Book,
KA LYMPUNG SAINDUR
lA KI PERSARA JINGSTAD.
The book is intended to help the teachers on the
different educational levels to improve the standard
of teaching and us it is, it will also be a great help to
the students under instruction to learn things more
scientifically and objectively.
This book contains a report on the Creative Art
Workshops as part of extension education project
conducted by the Centre for Creative Arts since its
inception in 1977, the programme being so devised
that the participants will benefit thiough different
lectures, demonstration, debating and extempore
competitions and composition tests besides widening
the horizons of knowledge upon the educational

inaccuracy, educational needs and assets. This is
the vita! role of Creative Art in education.
This publication is therefore, very much welcome
for educational improvement and I am sure the tea-
chers who use this book, will find it an immense plea-
sure in their work for more standards and accomplish-
ments and it should offer ample opportunity to the
students alike to improve in their studies.
We have published this book for easier distribu-
tion so that it should serve its purpose well as a help
book for teachers and students.
We thank Dr. H. Bareh and his staff for initia-
ting this publication on behalf of the University.
Shillong,
June, 1981.
S.C. Banwar
Acting Vice-Chancellor.
