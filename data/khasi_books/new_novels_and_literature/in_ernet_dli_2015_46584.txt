Dr Thorvald; Middle-aged, pedantic, would have been a

Colonel Hussek: An old lobster.

Vrodny-Hussek family has aristocratic traditions. 

Anna Vrodny: Must not be played as a mouse. She has 
character, but has hardly realized it. 

Oswald Vrodny: A cheerful ne’er-do-well. Might even speak
