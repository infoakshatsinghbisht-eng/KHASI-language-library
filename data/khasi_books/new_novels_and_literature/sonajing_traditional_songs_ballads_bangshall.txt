gitasa ku-marapo done scaha, aro uni mangsongani ba ortoara

ongani girain onga. Uamang ringo-chao, wano-ansengao, doroa- 
ajeao, krita-amuao aro grapa-mangtatao katta ring-e, sul sale ba 
katta dokesa an senga, mania aro ma ringa. ‘Gonda’- dokani,

mancharang ong a uni gimin '■‘Sonajing”-ko ua pilak sulrangna 
ma-keesa anga seaha. Ua A chik sulranga an-sengani, ma-ringani 
aro sinteani sulrang ongoba maikai kumarap katta-rikanina gimikan 
apsan ma-gapa uan A chik gisikoni nabaani aiao inmangipa 
manbatelaiani ong-a inc nikna man-a. 

Git ring-na ba sena changgipa sapgiparanga ba gitna gisik 
nangchongmotgiparanga mamung gipinrangna chanchiani dongna
