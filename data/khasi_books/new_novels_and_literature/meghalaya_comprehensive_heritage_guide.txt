Living Root Bridges of Cherrapunji 


Begin your journey in the quaint village of Cherrapunji, famous for its living root bridges, 


an ingenious marvel of traditional Khasi engineering. These ancient bridges, woven from 
the roots of the Ficus elastica tree, have stood the test of time, spanning across gushing 
rivers and verdant valleys. Trek through lush rainforests to discover these natural 


wonders, immersing yourself in the beauty of Meghalaya's pristine landscapes.

Venture to Nohkalikai Falls, the tallest plunge waterfall in India, where water cascades 
down a sheer cliff face into a turquoise pool below. Admire the breathtaking beauty of 
this natural wonder from the viewing platform, or embark on a trek to the base of the 
waterfall for a closer look. Feel the mist on your face and the roar of the falls echoing 


through the valley as you witness the sheer power and grandeur of nature. 


Mawlynnong - Asia's Cleanest Village 


Explore the pristine village of Mawlynnong, renowned as Asia's cleanest village, where 


winding footpaths meander through lush gardens and traditional Khasi huts dot the 
landscape. Marvel at the community's commitment to cleanliness and sustainability as 
you stroll through the village, admiring the picturesque scenery and interacting with 
friendly locals. Experience the warmth and hospitality of Meghalaya's rural life as you 


immerse yourself in the tranquility of this idyllic village. 


Dawki - Crystal Clear Waters of Umngot River 


Journey to Dawki, a picturesque town nestled along the banks of the Umngot River, 
renowned for its crystal-clear waters and breathtaking scenery. Take a boat ride on the 
emerald-green river, gliding past submerged rocks and sandy riverbeds, and marvel at 
the surreal beauty of the underwater world. Dive into the cool waters for a refreshing 
swim or simply relax on the riverbank and soak in the tranquility of this enchanting 


destination. 


Shillong - The Scotland of the East 


Discover the charming hill station of Shillong, often referred to as the Scotland of the 
East, where rolling hills, pine forests, and colonial architecture create a picturesque 
backdrop for exploration. Explore the bustling markets and vibrant cafes of Police 
Bazaar, visit the sacred Shillong Peak for panoramic views of the city, and immerse 


yourself in the vibrant culture and heritage of the Khasi people. 


Double Decker Living Root Bridge in Nongriat 


Embark on a trek to Nongriat village to marvel at the Double Decker Living Root Bridge, a 
unique natural wonder crafted by generations of Khasi villagers. Traverse through dense 
forests and steep valleys to reach this hidden gem, where two intertwining root bridges 
span a cascading waterfall, creating a surreal and breathtaking sight. Take a dip in the 


natural pools below or simply bask in the tranquility of this pristine wilderness. 


Cherrapunji - The Wettest Place on Earth 


Explore the legendary town of Cherrapunji, renowned as the wettest place on earth, 
where mist-covered cliffs, roaring waterfalls, and lush green valleys await to mesmerize 
and enchant. Visit the iconic Seven Sisters Waterfalls, explore the mysterious Mawsmai 
Cave, and trek to the scenic viewpoints of Eco Park for panoramic views of the 
surrounding landscape. Experience the magic of Meghalaya's monsoon season as you 


immerse yourself in the raw beauty of this rain-soaked paradise. 


Laitlum Canyon - The Grand Canyon of the East 


Journey to Laitlum Canyon, often referred to as the Grand Canyon of the East, where 
sheer cliffs plunge into verdant valleys and panoramic views stretch as far as the eye 
can see. Trek along rugged trails to reach the edge of the canyon, where breathtaking 
vistas of rolling hills and deep ravines unfold before you. Feel the rush of adrenaline as 


you peer over the edge and marvel at the sheer majesty of nature's handiwork. 


Balpakram National Park - Land of the Spirits 


Explore the mystical realm of Balpakram National Park, known as the Land of the Spirits, 
where dense forests, deep gorges, and sacred groves are said to be inhabited by 
supernatural beings. Trek through pristine wilderness to discover hidden waterfalls, 
ancient rock formations, and rare wildlife, including the elusive clouded leopard and the 
endangered Asian elephant. Experience the untamed beauty of Meghalaya's wild 


landscapes as you immerse yourself in the heart of this mystical wilderness. 


Krang Suri Falls - The Hidden Gem of Jaintia Hills 


Seek out the hidden gem of Krang Suri Falls, tucked away amidst the verdant hills of 
Jaintia Hills, where turquoise waters cascade down moss-covered rocks into a natural 
pool below. Trek through dense forests and meandering streams to reach this secluded 
paradise, where you can swim in the cool waters, picnic on the riverbank, or simply bask 
in the tranquility of nature's embrace. Feel the stress of the modern world melt away as 


you surrender to the beauty and serenity of this enchanting destination.
