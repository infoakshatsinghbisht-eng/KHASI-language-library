Activities of the Director of Employmeot and 
Craftsmen Training 

The Director of Employment and Craftsmen Training is 
responsible for the development and administration of the 
Programme relating to ibi Employment Service and Craftsmen 
Training in the State. The Di?ectorate functions through a net- 
work of Employment Exchanges and Industrial Training 
Institutes in the State. 

The Employment Exchanges register job-scekers with a 
view to rendering employment assistance. They also provide 
Vocational and General Guidance to students and job seekers. 
They are rcsj>onsible for the implementation of the Employment 
Exchanges (Compulsory Notification of Vacancies) Act, 1^)59 
which requires that every employer in the Public Sector and 
eniplojers in the Private Sector employing 25 or moie workers 
to notify vacancies occurring in their establishments and render 
employment returns to the Employment Exchange at fixed 
intervals. 

The Employment Officer also work in-close collaboration 
with various agencies for the promotion of self Employment. 
The Employment Exchanges are also utilised for guiding the 
applicant about the procedures for obtaining facilities and the 
agencies that would have to be approached for the purpose of 
self employment. 

At present, there are 5 (five) Emplo ment Exchanges in the 
State each one located in every District Headquarter. Besides, 
there are 7 (seven) Employment Information and Assistance 
Bureaux which are the extensions of the Employment Exchan- 
ges These Bureuax are located at Cherrapunjee, Mairang, 
Nongpoh, Khiiehriat, Betasing. Resubelpara and Dambuk- 
Aga to serve the interest of the people in the rural areas. 

There are 2 (two) Industrial Training Institutes, one at 
Shillong and the other at Tura. The Industrial Training Insti- 
tutes impart vocational training in different trades such as 
Stenography, Welder, Draughtsman (Civil), Electrician Motor 
Mechanic, Wireman Fitter and Carpentry. 

The Training session in the Industrial Training Institutes 
start from August every year. Stipends are awarded to 
Schedule Castes/Scheduled Tribes Trainees and ment stipend 
is awarded to other deserving candidates. 

The Directorate is also implementing the Apprentices Act, 
1951 where Apprentice may be engaged by various establish- 
ments in different trades located. 

Employers in both the Public and Private Sector are 
requested to (1) comply with the requirements of the Employ- 
ment Exchange (CNV) Act, 1959 (2) utilise the services of the 
Employment Exchanges and (3) utilise the skills of the local 
youths trained in the Industrial Training Institutes.
